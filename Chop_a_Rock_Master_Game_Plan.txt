00. INTRODUCTION
================================
Game Name:
Chop a Rock

1 DETAILED Paragraph Description of Game:
(Revised 2026-09-26 to reflect the two-axis progression change — see Decision Log.)
Chop a Rock is a Roblox game for young kids built around one satisfying action: winding up karate chops that whittle down a giant rock's health bar until it shatters (revised 2026-09-30: multi-chop rocks, see Decision Log). Players approach a boulder — a scaled cube part that grows through color and material tiers (gray → mossy → obsidian → glowing → crystalline → cosmic) — hold to wind up, and release as a meter passes through a power band. If you're not strong enough for that rock it just cracks and you earn nothing, a clear signal to go get stronger; if you are strong enough, the release timing scales the reward rather than gating it, so a sloppy release still breaks the rock for a small payout while a good one pays full and a perfect one triggers a bonus multiplier, a bigger shatter, and a louder crunch. Breaking rocks earns money, and money buys geodes that crack open to hatch cute PET ROCKS (revised 2026-09-30: pet rocks replace gems, see Decision Log). Progression runs on two axes: STRENGTH, raised by buying strict arm upgrades with money, which unlocks heavier rocks further out; and LUCK, which improves the rarity of hatched pet rocks. A few pet rocks can be equipped to follow the player and boost their gains; the rest are displayed on pedestals at the player's base, where they earn money while offline, and money buys more pedestal slots. Rebirthing resets strength and level to one and unlocks a new base floor. An index tracks every kind of rock broken and every pet rock hatched. Impact gets all the polish: screen shake, a slow-mo frame before contact, a giant damage number, and pre-fractured debris, with the player character held at fixed size so the boulder visibly dwarfs them. NPC crowds cheer and server-wide announcements fire on milestone breaks, because being seen being strong is half the fantasy. Icon-driven UI, huge buttons, minimal reading, monetized through gamepasses rather than randomized eggs.

Game Type:
Simulator / RNG

Main Player Fantasy:
Karate chopping a super big rock like an anime character.

Target Audience:
Ages 6–12.
[PROPOSED] Design rule: UI is designed for the youngest players (icons, colors, no reading required); progression depth is designed for the oldest players.

Main Selling Point:
You get to chop a really big rock and it is incredibly satisfying.
[PROPOSED] Sharper version: "The only simulator where every hit is a skill moment — built to look insane in a clip." (One skill-based, anime-impact action vs. the click-spam/auto-swing typical of the genre.)
[PROPOSED] Differentiating link: geodes are cracked with the chop timing meter, and timing quality affects hatch rarity (perfect crack = better odds), so skill feeds both progression axes.

Expectations:
Money printer.
Measurable target: 1,000 concurrent players (CCU).

MONETIZATION NOTE — must be resolved by Section 10:
Randomness now comes from hatching pet rocks out of geodes bought with in-game money (arms are strict upgrades). Remaining questions: may geodes be bought with Robux, and may Robux affect hatch luck (e.g., a "2x luck" gamepass)? Audience is 6–12; paid random items on Roblox require disclosed odds.

02. CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP
================================
Step #1: Train to build strength (idle allowed).
Step #2: Walk to a boulder.
Step #3: Hold to wind up the chop; release as the meter passes through the power band.
Step #4: If too weak → the rock cracks, no reward, session ends.
Step #5: If strong enough → each chop deals damage to the rock's health bar and pays its share of the rock's payout.
         The player stays in the chop stance; the meter resets with a new band position for the next chop,
         until the rock breaks or the player leaves. Timing sets damage and payout:
         sloppy = small, good = full, perfect = bonus multiplier. The breaking chop gets the full shatter for its quality
         (perfect = bigger shatter + louder crunch).
Step #6: Earn money.
Step #7: Spend money on the next arm upgrade (strict upgrade → more strength per training action), or on geodes that hatch pet rocks.
Step #8: Equip a few pet rocks to follow you (boosts); display the rest on base pedestals (offline money). Luck improves hatch rarity.
Step #9: With more strength, move further out to heavier rocks, which pay more money for better geodes.
Step #10: Buy more pedestal slots; eventually rebirth (strength + level reset to 1; new floor, multipliers, better training method).
Step #11: Repeat.

Loop details:
- Rock respawn: instant after breaking.
- One chop (wind-up, impact) takes about 2 seconds; one rock takes several chops, fewer the stronger the player is.
- Rock health: MaxHealth = StrengthRequired × HitsAtMinStrength. Damage per chop = Strength × quality damage multiplier.
  The health bar is mostly an indicator of player strength: a player far above the requirement one-shots the rock.
  [PLAYTEST] HitsAtMinStrength (draft 4), damage multipliers (draft Bad 0.4 / Mid 0.7 / Good 1 / Great 1.3 / Perfect 1.75).
- Payout per chop = BasePayout × quality payout multiplier × (damage dealt ÷ MaxHealth), capped at remaining health.
  BasePayout is per whole rock and was raised by HitsAtMinStrength so money per minute holds. [PLAYTEST]
- Health is per player, per session; it resets when the player leaves the rock.
- Moving power band: every chop, the server picks a random band center in [0.2, 0.8]. Bands nest around the center,
  shifting inward rather than clipping at the bar's ends. The meter shows the round's full band gradient, and a
  Chrome-style line moves along it to show where the hit will land.
  Because a centered band is crossed twice per cycle (two half-length windows instead of one), all non-Bad bands are
  widened by a zone width multiplier. [PLAYTEST] center range, multiplier (draft 1.5).
- Players move on when they have earned enough to do what's required to try the next rock.
- Players need a periodic indication of what to do next.

[PROPOSED] Next-goal bar: a persistent bar showing "your strength → next rock's requirement," plus an arrow/pulse when it fills.
[PROPOSED] ~0.5s reform animation on respawn instead of a true instant pop, so the break has a beat to land.
(Resolved: arms are now strict upgrades bought with money, so money converts predictably into strength. Pity system no longer needed.)

04. AI'S PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVE
================================
DIVISION OF LABOR:
- AI writes scripts.
- User does everything else (building, models, VFX, sounds, animations) until otherwise decided.
- UI: follows the boilerplate's AGENTS.md convention. ScreenGuis are authored in Studio's StarterGui and live in the place file (Rojo does not sync StarterGui). Screen modules under src/client/Controllers/UIController/Screens/ find elements by name inside the ScreenGui and drive them. If a Roblox Studio MCP is available, AI creates/edits ScreenGuis through it; otherwise AI writes the screen module and asks the user to author the ScreenGui in Studio. User must save the place after UI changes.

WORKFLOW:
- Rojo with files.
- Code standards are defined in the project's AGENTS.md. AI must read AGENTS.md before writing code.

OUTPUT STYLE:
- Whole files every time.
- Comments only when requested.
- AI must make NO changes outside the requested scope, even when outputting a whole file.
- User reviews git diffs before committing.

IDEA HANDLING:
- Anything player-facing: AI proposes and waits for approval.
- Internal implementation details (naming, internal structure) that follow AGENTS.md: AI may decide.

ASSET HANDOFF CONVENTION:
How AI-written scripts reference user-made assets.

1. Location: all user-made assets live under
     ReplicatedStorage/Assets/
       VFX/
       Sounds/
       Animations/
       Models/
       Images/
2. Naming: Category_Subject_Variant, PascalCase
     e.g. VFX_Shatter_Perfect, VFX_Aura_Obsidian, SFX_Chop_Yoo, Anim_Chop_Windup, Model_Rock_Mossy, Model_Pet_Pebbles, Model_Geode_Basic
3. Registry: scripts NEVER reference assets by path or ID directly. All lookups go through one ModuleScript
   (ReplicatedStorage/Shared/AssetRegistry) that maps keys to asset locations/IDs.
   Renaming or replacing an asset = one edit in the registry.
4. Placeholders: if an asset doesn't exist yet, AssetRegistry returns a placeholder (plain part, blank sound)
   and prints a warning naming the missing asset. The game must run with zero real assets.
5. Missing-asset list: when AI writes code that needs a new asset, it lists the asset name(s) it expects
   at the end of its response so the user knows what to make.
6. Rojo: the Assets folder is made in Studio, not in files. It must either be exported as .rbxm into the repo
   or marked $ignoreUnknownInstances in the Rojo project file, so syncing never wipes it.

06. WORLD, CONTENT, AND PLAYER INTERACTION
================================

WORLD TYPE:
Hub + zones. A central spawn hub, with a straight line of zones (one per rock tier) leading out from it (user's leaning: straight line).

WORLD DESCRIPTION:
- Spawn hub: a central training hub, ringed by player base plots.
- Training hub: where the intro cutscene and tutorial happen, and where all strength training takes place (including idle training).
  Hub aesthetic: mountain training grounds with sakura trees around (anime training-arc feel; bright, outdoor). Not a dojo.
- Base plots: fixed-pedestal bases arranged around the hub. Other players can walk in and view your pet rocks. A Home button teleports the player back to their base.
- Zones: a straight line leading out from the hub, one zone per rock tier. Each zone's environment matches its tier.
[PROPOSED] Zones separated by gates showing the required strength; breaking through fires a server announcement.
[PROPOSED] Teleport pad unlocks when a player first reaches a zone; players can jump to their highest zone from the hub.
[PROPOSED] Handcrafted tiers (gray → cosmic) form the line. Anything beyond cosmic uses teleports into reused/generated zones rather than extending the line (Roblox physics/rendering degrade far from the world origin).
Note: with training and bases at the hub, the server naturally gathers in one visible place; the zones are where players go to chop.

MAIN LOCATION / STARTING AREA:
The training hub in the spawn area. New players see the cutscene and tutorial there, then walk out into zone 1.
Rocks are per-player: each player sees and chops their own copy of each rock.
Base plots per server: 8 (this effectively sets max server size).

07. PROGRESSION, ECONOMY, AND REWARDS
================================

MAIN PROGRESSION METHOD:
Two axes:
- STRENGTH: gained by TRAINING, amplified by strict arm upgrades bought with money. Determines which rocks can be broken.
  - Training builds strength linearly.
  - Better arms increase the amount of strength gained per training action (arms act as a training-gain multiplier).
  - Idle (AFK) training is allowed.
  - Better training methods are unlocked by rebirthing.
  Training method: karate-themed board/brick breaking stacks (1 board → 5 boards → bricks → ice → steel), using the same timing meter as rock chops. Bench pressing rejected (signature mechanic of the lifting-simulator genre this redesign differentiates from); pushups rejected (no clean upgrade path).
  Idle/AFK training happens only while in-server. Money still earns offline via pet rocks displayed at the base; strength does not.
  [PROPOSED] Active timed training (good/perfect releases) trains faster than idle, so skill still pays.
- LUCK: improves the rarity of pet rocks hatched from geodes.
  [OPEN] Where luck comes from (rebirths, pet rock effects, upgrades, or a mix).
- PET ROCKS: hatched from geodes bought with money.
  - Equipped: a limited number follow the player and boost gains. [OPEN] Money, strength gain, or both. [PLAYTEST] Slot count, boost sizes.
  - Displayed: the rest sit on base pedestals and earn offline money.
Plus: base expansion (pedestal slots, floors) and rebirth.

PRIMARY CURRENCY: Money
How is it earned?
- Breaking rocks (payout scaled by timing quality: sloppy / good / perfect).
- Offline income from pet rocks displayed at the base.
What is it spent on?
- Arm upgrades (strength).
- Geodes (pet rocks).
- Additional pedestal slots at the base.

SECONDARY CURRENCY:
None defined.

PREMIUM CURRENCY, if any:
None defined.

UPGRADES:
- Arms: strict tier upgrades → more strength; each tier has a unique look + aura.
- Base: more pedestal slots (money); more floors (rebirth).

ITEM / EQUIPMENT TIERS:
- Arm tiers (strict, sequential).
- Pet rocks (collection; equipped or displayed at base).
- Geodes (consumable; hatch one pet rock).

RARITIES:
Pet rocks have rarities. Luck changes the odds.
[PROPOSED] Geode tiers per zone (pricier geodes further out have better odds), replacing "bigger rocks drop rarer gems."
[OPEN] Number of rarities and their names.
[OPEN] Odds table (must be displayable to players).

UNLOCK REQUIREMENTS:
- Rocks/zones: strength.
- Base floors: rebirth.

PLAYER LEVELS:
Cosmetic only (no gameplay effect). Resets to 1 on rebirth.

XP SYSTEM:
EXP bar is filled by strength. Level is effectively a display of strength progress.

REWARDS:
What activities give rewards? Breaking rocks (money); hatching geodes (pet rocks); pet rocks at base (offline money).
What determines reward amount? Rock size/tier, timing quality, equipped pet boosts; luck (for hatch rarity).
[PROPOSED] Geodes are cracked with the chop meter; timing quality improves hatch odds.
How does risk affect reward? Attempting a rock that's too strong = crack, no reward.

REBIRTH:
- Resets strength and level to 1.
- Unlocks a new base floor (more pedestal space).
- Grants multipliers to money gain and strength gain.
[OPEN] What else resets? Money? Arm tier (since arms = strength)? Pedestal slots?
       → [PLAYTEST] User will decide through playtesting. Code must support either choice via config.
[OPEN] What persists? Presumably pet rocks, index, and floors — confirm.
[PLAYTEST] Do rebirth multipliers stack per rebirth (additive/multiplicative), and by how much?

OFFLINE INCOME:
- Pet rocks displayed at the base earn money while the player is offline.
[OPEN] Offline cap (e.g., max hours accrued) — without one, offline income can outpace active play.
[PROPOSED] Active chopping should always earn clearly more per minute than offline income, so playing beats idling.

COLLECTION / INDEX:
- Tracks every kind of rock broken and every pet rock hatched.
[OPEN] What happens to duplicate pet rocks? (Sell, fuse, keep, discard?)
[OPEN] Rewards for completing index entries/pages?

GENERAL PROGRESSION FLOW:
Base chop
↓
Train → strength (linear; idle allowed)
Break rocks → money
↓
Buy arm upgrades (training-gain multiplier) ← money
Buy geodes → hatch pet rocks (luck improves rarity) ← money
Equip pet rocks (boosts); display the rest on pedestals (offline money)
↓
Break bigger rocks → more money → better geodes
↓
Buy more pedestal slots
↓
Rebirth → strength + level reset to 1; new floor, money/strength-gain multipliers, better training method unlocked
↓
Repeat with more luck, base capacity, and faster training

[OPEN] What happens after the cosmic tier? Does rebirth replace "infinite" rock tiers, or do tiers continue past cosmic?
