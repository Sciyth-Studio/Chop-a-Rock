# Cannon Shop

Spec: [[Cannons And Auras Replace Arms]]. Built 2026-10-20 with placeholder models; the real lobby cannon went in the same day (see World); the real stall and per-zone cannon models still come from `model-workflow`.

## Config (`CannonConfig`, all drafts)
| Cannon | Strength | Price | Colour | Marker |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Desert | 500 | $80K | 255,170,0 | `TeleportPoints.CannonDesert` |
| Jungle | 62,500 | $5.1M | 0,210,40 | `CannonJungle` |
| Ocean | 7.81M | $328M | 0,90,255 | `CannonOcean` |
| Snow | 976.56M | $21B | 140,220,255 | `CannonSnow` |
Strength = the zone's first rock; price = about 25 of its chops. Matches the Config and Payback tabs of [[Balance Model]] v7 (each cannon costs 3 to 6 min of income in the zone before it). Flight 2 s, arc height max(60, 25% of distance), cooldown 3 s, prompt range 10. No-cannon colour grey 120.

## Data and server
- `PlayerData.Cannons` (`{ [key]: true }`) and `EquippedCannon` (`""` = none). Kept through rebirth (user, 2026-10-20). In admin snapshots.
- `CannonService`: `Buy` (not owned, strength and cash checked on the server, then equips it), `Equip(key)` (`""` unequips; must own), `SetOwned` (admin), `IsLaunching`, `Find`. Packets `BuyCannon(key)`, `EquipCannon(key)`.
- Launch (since 2026-10-21, user: "moving the cannon onto the back wall of the lobby and using the shop to fire yourself, the main area of the lobby is cluttered"): no prompt on the cannon any more. `FireCannon(key)` from the shop's Fire button (must own it) sets `EquippedCannon` to that key (it now only means "last fired", for the barrel tint and the card outline), then checks alive, not revealing a hatch, not already flying, cooldown, ends every activity through `TeleportService:EndActivities`, streams the destination in, fires `CannonLaunch(player, key, start, end, height, chargeSeconds, flightSeconds)` to every client and after the 0.5 s charge plus the flight `PivotTo`s the character onto the marker (server-authoritative landing). `TeleportService` refuses teleports while a flight is running. No distance check: firing works wherever the shop is open. Before 2026-10-21 a "Fire / Cannon" ProximityPrompt sat on the cannon and with nothing equipped opened the shop (`CannonEmpty`, removed with `EquipCannon`).
- Client snap: when the character is more than `PullRangeStuds` (30) from the `Bore`, the charge starts with the character already inside the barrel instead of pulling it across the lobby.
