# Item icons

Built 2026-10-23 from `IconSystemPlan.md` (D1–D6 approved that day, look per [[Rok Icon Reference]]). The procedure lives in the `item-icons` skill. Rendering facts are in [[Item Icon Rendering]].

- **Runtime:** `src/shared/Utils/ItemIcons.luau`. `ItemIcons.Apply(holder, "Pet" | "Rock", key, buildFallback)` shows the holder's `Icon` ImageLabel when `ItemIconConfig` has an id. Otherwise it shows `Preview` (ViewportFrame) and builds the 3D model as before. It returns whichever it showed, and `ItemIcons.Tint` sets `ImageColor3` on it, so silhouettes (`HiddenColor`) work on both.
- **Screens:** `Index.luau` (cards and detail pane), `GeodeStand.luau` (cards), and since 2026-10-24 `Roks.luau` (pedestal rows) and `SellShop.luau` (cards). Every Rok shown in a UI list now uses its icon. HatchReveal keeps its 3D model (D5, re-confirmed by the user 2026-10-24), and the Shop arm preview is unchanged. Pedestals and held Roks are real world models, not UI. No HUD, notification or billboard shows a Rok preview.
- **Modifier tints:** Toasty and Eclipsed recolour the 3D model. On icons the user chose (2026-10-24) to multiply the image instead: `RokModifier.ImageTint(modifier)` gives white lerped toward the tint by `TintAlpha`, set with `ItemIcons.Tint`. It also tints the eyes and is a bit darker than the 3D lerp; accepted.
- **Studio:** `Icon` ImageLabels (Fit, hidden, same size and position as `Preview`) were added 2026-10-23 to `StarterGui.Index…CardTemplate`, `StarterGui.Index.PopRoot.Panel.Detail` and `StarterGui.GeodeStand…CardTemplate`, and 2026-10-24 to `StarterGui.Roks…RowTemplate` (copies the Preview's dark background and grey `UIStroke`) and `StarterGui.SellShop…CardTemplate`. The place must be saved for them to stick.
- **Ids:** `src/shared/Config/ItemIconConfig.luau` is generated by `scripts/upload_icons.py` from `assets/Icons/uploaded.json` (D4). Uploads go to group 10962733 with the existing `ROBLOX_API_KEY` (the user chose it over a separate icons key, 2026-10-23).
- **Coverage:** all 5 zones: 30 Roks and 15 rocks, 45 icons in `assets/Icons/` and `contact_sheet.png`. The forest nine came first (D6). The other 36 followed on 2026-10-24, after the zone rock recolour ([[Zones After Desert]]). A new Rok or rock falls back to 3D until it's added to the manifest and uploaded.
- **Exclusive Roks (2026-10-25):** Champ (rbxassetid://106981353856481) and Kingy (rbxassetid://117002415230870) were added, making 47 icons. The Robux shop's Kingy card, the starter pack's Champ tile and the gift picker read them through `ShopCards.PetIcon`, so they stop falling back to `IconConfig.Crown`. The VIP card's crown is meant to stay. There's no Index Exclusive row in the code yet. Their off-white bodies (240, 235, 220) come out near pure white in the bright icon look, so the studs are faint; left as is. Seen in Play in the Roks panel. See [[Exclusive Rok Sheets]].
- **Status 2026-10-24:** all 45 uploaded (ids in `ItemIconConfig`). Playtested: every Roks Index card uses its icon with no fallbacks, all 45 ids load in an ImageLabel test, and the Geode Stand Snow tab shows its icons. `ContentProvider:PreloadAsync` on the raw id strings reports `Failure` even for icons that display, so use ImageLabel `IsLoaded` to check them. The black outline blends into the dark card backgrounds.
