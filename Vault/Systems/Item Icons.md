# Item icons

Built 2026-10-23 from `IconSystemPlan.md` (D1–D6 approved that day, look per [[Pet Icon Reference]]). The procedure lives in the `item-icons` skill. Rendering facts are in [[Item Icon Rendering]].

- **Runtime:** `src/shared/Utils/ItemIcons.luau`. `ItemIcons.Apply(holder, "Pet" | "Rock", key, buildFallback)` shows the holder's `Icon` ImageLabel when `ItemIconConfig` has an id. Otherwise it shows `Preview` (ViewportFrame) and builds the 3D model as before. It returns whichever it showed, and `ItemIcons.Tint` sets `ImageColor3` on it, so silhouettes (`HiddenColor`) work on both.
- **Screens:** `Index.luau` (cards and detail pane), `GeodeStand.luau` (cards), and since 2026-10-24 `Pets.luau` (pedestal rows) and `SellShop.luau` (cards). Every Pet shown in a UI list now uses its icon. HatchReveal keeps its 3D model (D5, re-confirmed by the user 2026-10-24), and the Shop arm preview is unchanged. Pedestals and held Pets are real world models, not UI. No HUD, notification or billboard shows a Pet preview.
- **Modifier tints:** Toasty and Eclipsed recolour the 3D model. On icons the user chose (2026-10-24) to multiply the image instead: `PetModifier.ImageTint(modifier)` gives white lerped toward the tint by `TintAlpha`, set with `ItemIcons.Tint`. It also tints the eyes and is a bit darker than the 3D lerp; accepted.
- **Studio:** `Icon` ImageLabels (Fit, hidden, same size and position as `Preview`) were added 2026-10-23 to `StarterGui.Index…CardTemplate`, `StarterGui.Index.PopRoot.Panel.Detail` and `StarterGui.GeodeStand…CardTemplate`, and 2026-10-24 to `StarterGui.Pets…RowTemplate` (copies the Preview's dark background and grey `UIStroke`) and `StarterGui.SellShop…CardTemplate`. The place must be saved for them to stick.
- **Ids:** `src/shared/Config/ItemIconConfig.luau` is generated by `scripts/upload_icons.py` from `assets/Icons/uploaded.json` (D4). Uploads go to group 10962733 with the existing `ROBLOX_API_KEY` (the user chose it over a separate icons key, 2026-10-23).
- **Coverage:** all 5 zones: 30 Pets and 15 rocks, 45 icons in `assets/Icons/` and `contact_sheet.png`. The forest nine came first (D6). The other 36 followed on 2026-10-24, after the zone rock recolour ([[Zones After Desert]]). A new Pet or rock falls back to 3D until it's added to the manifest and uploaded.
- **Exclusive Pets (2026-10-25):** Champ (rbxassetid://106981353856481) and Kingy (rbxassetid://117002415230870) were added, making 47 icons. The Robux shop's Kingy card, the starter pack's Champ tile and the gift picker read them through `ShopCards.PetIcon`, so they stop falling back to `IconConfig.Crown`. The VIP card's crown is meant to stay. There's no Index Exclusive row in the code yet. Their off-white bodies (240, 235, 220) come out near pure white in the bright icon look, so the studs are faint; left as is. Seen in Play in the Pets panel. See [[Exclusive Pet Sheets]].
- **Animal Pets (2026-11-03):** 32 more icons uploaded for the animals in [[Animal Pets]], making 79, and all 32 load in Studio. The user chose to upload them as they are, keeping the approved near-front camera, even though it hides some features: the Unicorn's snout and mane, the Gecko's spots and tail, the Panther's rosettes, the Armadillo's snout and the Squirrel's tail. The Yeti also washes out. A per-icon camera angle and darker colours on those would be the fix, if the user wants it later. `specs.json` gained a `textureFile` field for the Tiger and Panther bodies, which the renderer now reads. The icons were re-rendered and re-uploaded without studs on 2026-11-04, when the user chose no studs on animals. The rock Pet icons stay until the config swap.
- **Animal Pets with studs (2026-11-04):** all 62 animals were re-rendered and uploaded with `Animal Stud` and approved by the user; 30 of them are new, making 109 icons, and all 62 load in an ImageLabel test. The zone 6-10 Pets show nothing until their config exists. The stud variant's new `flat` kind in `specs.json` multiplies the part colour by the tile with no gain or bump. The lights clipped pale colours to flat white, hiding the shape and eyes (the user caught this on the white Pets), so `soften_pale` in the renderer darkens near-white and cream on parts with `Animal Stud` or a `textureFile` (`pale_*` in `STYLE`). Rock and rock Pet icons and all eyes are unchanged. Open: the Koi icon has a black square and triangle on its whiskers. They aren't from the whisker colour or the body texture, and the FBX has no holes, flipped faces or broken normals; they haven't been compared with the Studio model.
- **Status 2026-10-24:** all 45 uploaded (ids in `ItemIconConfig`). Playtested: every Pets Index card uses its icon with no fallbacks, all 45 ids load in an ImageLabel test, and the Geode Stand Snow tab shows its icons. `ContentProvider:PreloadAsync` on the raw id strings reports `Failure` even for icons that display, so use ImageLabel `IsLoaded` to check them. The black outline blends into the dark card backgrounds.
