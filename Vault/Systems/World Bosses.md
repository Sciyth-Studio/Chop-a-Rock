# World bosses

First playable slice of `WorldBossPlan.md` (build order steps 2 and part of 5). Claude playtested the Big Croc on 2026-11-11; the user hasn't seen it yet. All numbers are drafts in `BossConfig`.

## How a fight runs (`BossService`)
- **Spawn:** admin "Spawn Boss" (alias `boss`, choice BigCroc / BoulderCrab / FrostYeti; no choice = random, never the last one). Clones `Model_Boss_<Key>` onto `BossArena.DiamondSpot`, turned 180 degrees so the front faces the gateway. Model `Workspace.WorldBoss`, tag `WorldBoss`, Persistent streaming. Animations are played on the server (`AnimationController.Animator`), so every client sees them.
- **Not with the Diamond Rock:** both use `DiamondSpot`, so each refuses to spawn while the other is out (the boss clock dropped a Diamond onto the Croc in the first test). The event clock does **not** roll bosses yet.
- **Phases** (`BossConfig.Phases`, model attribute `Phase`): Sleeping 30 s (Sleep loop, label "Wakes up in 0:30") → Waking 3 s (Wake clip) → Fighting, 4:00 (Idle loop) → at half health **Mad** for 3 s (Mad clip, eyes and pupils turn Neon red, timer resets to 5:00, every 3rd round a Block round, Mad difficulty, 30 HP per miss) → Fighting → at 10% **Finish** 6 s (Dizzy loop, every spot x2, no Block rounds) → **Defeated** (Defeat clip, removed after 3 s) or **Leaving** on time-out (removed after 2 s; no walk-off animation yet).
- In half 1 damage can't take the boss below half (`Fight.Apply` floors it), so the mad step can't be skipped.
- **Health:** each player's share = max(their strength, best zone's first rock) x Good damage x `HealthChops` (100), so about 100 Good chops each. Everyone out of the tutorial is counted at spawn; anyone else is added on their first chop, keeping the bar's percentage (`Fight.AddShare`). The first try used the best zone's top rock strength and gave a strong save about 1,000 hits.
- **Weak spots:** `WeakSpot1`-`4`, 4 slots each (`SpotIndexAttribute` `BossSpot` 1-4). Prompt "Chop" on each (client), packet `BossChop(spot)`. The server sets on each spot `ChopScale` (1.5), `ChopNormal` (world, flattened, from the rig's `Normal` attribute), `ChopGroundY` (floor) and `ChopFanSlots` / `ChopFanDegrees` (4, 60). `ChopController` stands the player in a 60-degree fan in front of the spot, on the floor, and keeps the over-the-shoulder camera (as on the Diamond).
- **The weak spot:** model attribute `GoldSpot`, moves every 20 s ("Weak spot moved!"), x2 damage. Only it glows (its own Neon glow colour); the other three take the boss's `SkinColor` with Plastic + `2022 Stud` (user 2026-11-11: "the spots that aren't weak should be the same color as the skin"), and all four are skin while asleep. They can still be chopped at x1. The dark pentagon frame around each spot is baked into the limb mesh, so it still shows on skin-coloured spots.
- **Pay per chop** (`Fight.RoundPay`): what that chop would pay on the top rock of your best zone (that rock's payout x the quality's payout multiplier x min(1, your damage / its health)) x `RoundCashMultiplier` 2.1 (Balance Model v18 H16), then friend, Index and pass multipliers. Analytics source `Boss`. Master uses the Master payout. Damage is separate: strength x the quality's damage, x2 gold or Finish, x1.5 after a perfect block.
- **Block rounds:** every 4th round (3rd when mad) during Fighting, same packets and judge as [[Block Rounds]]. The boss plays `Attack` (at most every 2.5 s). Missed: -20 HP (-30 mad). Perfect: +10 HP, a counter hit of one Great's damage, and x1.5 on your next chop.
- **Eyes shut while asleep (user 2026-11-11):** in Sleeping, `BossController` squashes the `Eyes` part to `SleepEyeScale` (0.12) of its height in a darker skin colour (`LidShade` 0.35 toward black) and hides `Pupils`; on wake they pop open with a 0.35 s Back tween. Client-only.
- **Fight HP:** player attribute `BossHP` (100). At 0: chop ends, "Knocked out!", attribute `BossKnockedOut` for 5 s (prompts off), then teleport to `BossArena.GateSpot` (new invisible marker inside the gateway, 42, 1.1, -478) with full HP. No flop or stars yet.
- **End:** win: everyone who dealt at least 3% of their own share gets the win bonus (48 Good rounds of pay) and "Big Croc beaten! +$X". Time-out after the mad half: anyone who hit it gets 25% of the win bonus ("Almost!"). Then "got bored" (half 1) or "ran away" (half 2).
- **Admin (Events tab):** Spawn Boss, Boss: Wake Now (`bosswake`), Boss: -25% Health (`bosshit`), Boss: 10s Left (`bossend`).

## Fixed along the way
`ChopResult`'s payout was `NumberU32`, so any chop paying over about $4.3B showed a wrapped number on the client (rocks too). It is `NumberF64` now.

- **Server (`BossService`, `Attacks.luau`):** `fight.Busy` blocks chop requests, releases and new rounds; every chop ends 0.7 s later through the normal `ChopEnded`, so the last hit still shows. Model attributes `Attack` (0 none, 1 Stomp, 2 Big), `AttackLandsAt` (server time), `AttackCenter`, `StompRadius`, and for a Big Attack the hazard written by `ReplicatedStorage.BossHazards` (`HazardStyle`, `HazardOrigin`, `HazardAngle`, `HazardSpan`, `HazardReach`, `HazardSize`, `HazardPoint1-16`). Pockets go 20-45 studs from players who don't already have one within 45 (40 apart, at least 30 from the yeti, 14 in from the edge), and the rest go randomly. `HazardPoint1-3` are the pocket centres, `HazardSize` the radius, `HazardReach` 150. The server judges `SpreadSeconds` (0.5) after landing, when the ice has reached the far edge (`Attacks.JudgeDelay`). Pockets count 11% bigger (`JudgeScale` 0.9), and knock-back is straight away from the yeti. `BossHazards.IsHit` is the one test both sides use. Big plays `BigAttack` at once (`Hit` at 4.0 s = the 4 s countdown); Stomp plays `Stomp` 3 s in (`Hit` at 1.0 s). On landing the server judges everyone in the arena (150 studs each way from the centre, not knocked out, out of the tutorial): the danger shapes count 3% (fan) or 10% (lines, spots) smaller, the stomp ring 5% smaller. Knock-back follows the shape: along the tail's swing, sideways off a line, or away from a boulder. Safe at a Big Attack: +10 HP and x1.5 on your next chop (`NextBonus`, used by your next session's first chop). Hit: -40 (Big) or -20 (Stomp) HP, a knock-back sent as `BossAttackResult(outcome, vector)`, and a knock-out at 0 HP. 1.5 s (Big) or 1.2 s (Stomp) later, "Back to chopping!".

## User playtest notes, 2026-11-12 (built, check.ps1 passes, not playtested)
- **Pay on completion (user: "giving players money for every hit doesn't make sense"):** a chop no longer pays. Its `Fight.RoundPay` goes into `fight.Pending[player]` and `ChopResult` carries 0, so there's no cash pop on boss hits. Win: pending + the win bonus (bonus only if you dealt `WinCreditShare` 3% of your share; below that, pending only, "Name: +$X"). Time-out after mad: `AlmostShare` 25% of (pending + win bonus). Time-out before mad: nothing. Friend, Index and pass multipliers apply once at the end. The Boss Chest reward pool is still the user's call.
- **Down longer:** `DefeatSeconds` 15 (was 7) before the beaten boss is removed; it holds the Defeat clip's last frame the whole time.
- **Harder Perfects when mad:** the Perfect band is `MadPerfectScale` 0.65 of its width while the boss's `IsMad` is set. The server snapshots it per round (`session.PerfectScale`); `ChopBar` reads the boss model's `IsMad` when it paints each round. `ChopZones.Bands` / `Classify` take an optional `perfectScale`.
- **Bigger boss bar:** `BossBarConfig.BarScale` 1.25 through the Body's `PopScale`, with the Root moved down by the extra half-height so it stays on screen. Applies to the Diamond Rock too.
