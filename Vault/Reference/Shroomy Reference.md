# Shroomy reference

Shroomy is the Rare forest Rok in the [[Master Game Plan]] §03 lineup (sheet received 2026-10-04).

![[Shroomy Reference Sheet.webp]]

Take from it:
- Body: [[Bloky Reference]]'s chamfered block, but taller and shallower (about 2 wide x 2.2 tall x 1.5 deep studs). Same chamfered-square eyes on the front, plain grey.
- Big mushroom: centred on the top face, faceted red cap about two thirds of the body width with 4 to 5 white square spots, short cream-white stem.
- Small top mushroom: same look at about 40% size, on the top face toward the right and back (seen from the front).
- Two small mushrooms grow sideways out of the body's left face (seen from the front), one above the other, a little behind centre; right side and back are clean.
- Caps are low-poly domes with a flat underside; spots are small white squares standing slightly proud of the cap.
- Mushrooms are separate meshes by colour (caps red, stems cream, spots white) so colours stay on the parts; no studs on them.
- Front is the eyes view (top-left of the sheet).
