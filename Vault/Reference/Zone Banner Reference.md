# Zone banner reference

![[Zone Banner Reference.png]]

The user (2026-11-09, feedback item 2): remove the zone text signs and show this every time a player walks into a zone, through the notification system, with no sound.

Take from it:
- A dark translucent strip fading out at both ends, across the top middle of the screen.
- Zone name large, in a green gradient with a dark stroke, followed by an emoji.
- A smaller line under it: "Recommended:" in white with a black stroke, then an icon and the value in the stat's colour (the reference shows a shoe and "5 Speed" in blue).
