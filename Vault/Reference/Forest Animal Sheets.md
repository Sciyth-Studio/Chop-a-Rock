Take from them:
- **Bunny:** tall upright rounded ears with pink inners, a small white snout with a pink nose, four white feet, a white puff tail.
- **Squirrel:** small ears with pink inners and dark tufts, a cream snout and chest, cream feet, and one huge tail curling up over the back.
- **Owl:** pointed feather tufts, cream rims round the eyes, a yellow beak, a cream belly with dark V marks, darker side wings, a small fan tail, two yellow feet.
- **Bear:** round ears with tan inners, a tan snout and chest, stubby side arms, a small ball tail, dark feet, and glowing honey on top dripping down the front and back.
- **Unicorn:** a stacked glowing gold horn, white ears with pink inners, a white snout with grey nostrils, a chunky pink, purple and blue mane running into the tail, gold feet.
