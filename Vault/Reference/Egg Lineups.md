## Lineup A: Forest to Snow
![[Egg Lineup A.webp]]
Top: ForestMossy, DesertMesa, JungleVine, OceanReef, SnowCap. Bottom: ForestMushroom, DesertCactus, JungleTiki, OceanBarnacle, SnowIcicle.

- **ForestMossy:** moss cap covers the top third, with long uneven drips.
- **DesertMesa:** bands step in at each colour change; a small grass tuft on a flat top.
- **JungleVine:** a thick vine spirals around, with leaves and yellow flowers.
- **OceanReef:** the coral is taller than the plan's limit, so shorten it. Flat starfish on the side.
- **SnowCap:** ignore the stray white oval on the right.
- **ForestMushroom:** raised white discs, two small mushrooms at the front base.
- **DesertCactus:** the cacti stick out above the top; keep them inside the height limit.
- **JungleTiki:** square brows, nose wedge, wide mouth, raised rather than carved.
- **OceanBarnacle:** hollow-topped cones of mixed sizes.
- **SnowIcicle:** a wide collar overhangs the lower half, with icicles below it.
