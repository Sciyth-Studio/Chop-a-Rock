# Zone rocks reference

Made from Claude's prompts, one sheet per zone. These are for shape and props only. Body colours are still being decided, see [[Zones After Desert]].

## Desert
![[Desert Rocks Reference.png]]
The sheet has 5 columns: Sandstone, then Canyon Boulder twice (yellow and orange), then Cactus Boulder twice. Sandstone: layered mini mesa with a grass tuft. Canyon: stepped ledges, a skull on a ledge, pink flowers at the base. Cactus: a big rounded boulder with two saguaros.

## Jungle
![[Jungle Rocks Reference.webp]]
Fern Rock: a lumpy rock with a fern cluster on top. Vine Boulder: a rounded boulder with thick vines and yellow flowers. Tiki Boulder: a tall egg shape with carved faces on two sides and palm leaves on top. Here the tiki face is on the front, not the right side as the prompt asked.

## Ocean
![[Ocean Rocks Reference.webp]]
Barnacle Rock: cone barnacles all over. Kelp Boulder: flat kelp strands draped over the top like a mop. Reef Boulder: a low, wide slab with coral, anemones and a starfish. The views are incomplete and out of order.

## Snow
![[Snow Rocks Reference.png]]
Snowy Rock: a round rock with a snow cap. Drift Boulder: a tall wedge with a sloped snow top and icicles. Igloo Boulder: a dome with a snow-brick roof and a doorway. The layout is messy, and the bottom-right shows Drift and Igloo at their relative scale.

## Zones 6-10 (2026-10-31)
Each sheet has one row per rock (First, Second, Third). The columns are front, back, left, right, top and 3/4. The user says the front doesn't matter for these rocks, because they can be chopped from any direction. The body colours on the sheets are only a guide: the approved colours are in [[World 1 Plan]] ([[Realistic Rock Bodies]]). All 15 are batch-modelled in one `assets/Rocks/World1Rocks.blend`.

### Swamp
![[Swamp Rocks Reference.webp]]
Muddy Rock has a thick mud cap over its top with blobby drips down the sides, and a reed tuft at one side of the base. Reedy Boulder has two cattails plus grass blades on top, and a lily pad with a pink flower at the base. Rooty Boulder has thick roots that start at a knot on top and branch down every side into the ground.

### Savanna
![[Savanna Rocks Reference.webp]]
Grassy Rock has spiky dry grass tufts on top and at two base corners. Acacia Boulder has a thin forked trunk on top with a flat canopy made of three hexagonal pads. Anthill Boulder has one tall termite spire with four short ones around it, off-centre on top.

### Bamboo
![[Bamboo Rocks Reference.webp]]
Shooty Rock has four cone shoots on top with a ring at each segment. Bamboo Boulder has five stalks of different heights, with node rings, cut tops and a few leaves. Pagoda Boulder has a pale stone lantern on top with **two** red roof tiers (not three) and a dark window.

### Volcano
![[Volcano Rocks Reference.webp]]
Smoky Rock has dark crack grooves across its top and smoke from the top. Ember Boulder has a network of glowing orange cracks over the whole surface. Crater Boulder is a truncated cone with a raised rim and a glowing pool in the bowl, with smoke.

### Ruins
![[Ruins Rocks Reference.webp]]
Rune Rock is tall and egg-shaped, with inset glowing purple runes (a circle, triangles and bars) all around it. Pillar Boulder has a broken fluted column leaning on top and a fallen column chunk beside it. Statue Boulder has a big stone fist, about half the body's height, rising from its top, knuckles forward.
