Take from it:
- **Body:** Bloky's chamfered cube with nothing changed, acting as head and body.
- **Parts:** two tall ears with black tips and white inners, a short white snout with a black square nose, a white chest band low on the front, four stubby black block feet, and a big faceted orange tail with a white tip, rising from the lower back.
- **Colours:** vivid orange, clean white, black, in flat areas with faceted shading only.
- **Eyes:** use Bloky's real meshes. The sheet draws them a little bigger and thinner-rimmed than Bloky's, so don't copy that.
- **Tail:** the back view shows it off to one side, and the side views show it behind the body. The model sits it behind and slightly to the Fox's right.
