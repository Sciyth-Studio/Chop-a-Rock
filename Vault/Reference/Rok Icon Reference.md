# Rok icon reference

The user shared this on 2026-10-23, during the icon spike, as the look they want for Rok icons. It refines D1 in `IconSystemPlan.md` (which said "the current preview look plus a black outline").

![[Rok Icon Reference.webp]]

Take from it:
- Camera almost straight on, slightly from the upper left, so a little of the top and left side shows. Mild perspective.
- Bright, soft studio lighting: top faces lightest, left faces a touch darker, soft shadow under each stud. Blacks stay deep black.
- Studs are real raised square studs with bevelled edges. Whole studs sit inside each face, inset from the edges, and none are cut.
- A thick black outline (about 2% of the image) around the whole silhouette, including where studs stick out at the edges. Round outer corners, no drop shadow.

Don't take: the bear itself (Roks stay rocks, see [[Roks Stay Rocks]]) or its white colour. Colours come from the Studio parts.
