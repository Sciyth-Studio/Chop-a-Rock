# Event timer icons reference (2026-11-09)

From the user's 2026-11-09 feedback list (item 5). Both screenshots are from another game.

![[Event Strip To Remove 2026-11-09.png]]
What not to build: a wide strip with the event name as a big title ("WORLD TREE"), a long progress bar and the time under its middle. The user said weather will no longer use the large strip to say when it starts, and won't notify 5 minutes before.

![[Event Timer Icons 2026-11-09.png]]
The target: one small icon per upcoming event, side by side (here a tree and a falling meteor rock with purple crystals), each with its countdown right under it ("03:38", "01:32") in white bold italic with a thick black stroke, two-digit minutes. No background panel, no title text. The icons are the flat chunky [[Game Icon Pack]] style.
