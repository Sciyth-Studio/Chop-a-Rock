---
description: Player-facing names (pet modifiers etc.) are simple kid words and never reuse a rock material name.
type: preference
---
Names players see, such as pet rock weather modifiers, should be simple one-word kid words ("Windy", not "Windswept").

They must never be a rock material name (sand, lava/molten, ice/frozen, crystal, obsidian...). Future zones will have rocks made of those materials, and a "Molten" pet would get confused with lava rocks. The visual effects follow the same rule: a modifier's effect must not look like a rock material.

"Diamond" is reserved for the Diamond Rock, the shared server-wide special rock (2026-10-06): no zone, pet or modifier uses it.

Came up 2026-10-03 when naming the weather modifiers (Dusty, Windy, Toasty, Zappy, Eclipsed). See [[Master Game Plan]] §05.
