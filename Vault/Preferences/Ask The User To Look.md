# Ask the user to look

2026-10-15, during the weather VFX pass: "if you need to look at something just ask me."

- When judging an effect needs many screenshot rounds (motion, timing, whether something shows at all), take one or two stills to catch obvious problems, then ask the user to watch it in Studio and say what's off.
- Also ask when something is easier to see than to probe: a GUI that `screen_capture` can't show, a click Claude's test mouse can't reach, or a playtest moment.
