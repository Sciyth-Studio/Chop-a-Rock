# Pets stay rocks

**Superseded 2026-11-03 by [[Animal Pets]]:** the user chose blocky animals. This note only describes the rock Pets until the swap ships. Don't apply it to new Pets.

The user doesn't want Pets to drift into "animal but rock" territory (2026-10-04). A first pet lineup had an owl rock, a deer rock, a tortoise, a scorpion and cactus arms, and the user pushed back.

How to apply:
- The body is always a rock. Don't give it legs, arms, wings, tails, claws, beaks, ears or an animal head.
- The face is only [[Bloky Reference]]'s chamfered-square eyes.
- Zone theme and rarity come from things growing on, sitting on or worn by the rock (plants, hats, objects), from rock formations (stacks, arches, carved stone) and from Neon accents.
- Exception (user, 2026-10-16): the ocean's Glowy gets fish gills, drawn as slits cut into the rock with glowing frills. It is the only Pet with an animal part so far. Don't add more unless the user asks.
- Pets match their zone. Pet names follow [[Simple Names No Material Clash]].

The 30-pet lineup (five zones, six each) is in the [[Master Game Plan]] §03.
