His face doesn't need to show. The Bacon Hair is instantly recognisable on Roblox, so a full back view is fine.

Studs appear only on the ground (a baseplate) in icons and thumbnails, if at all. The character, rocks and props stay smooth.

**Why:** the user said so on 2026-10-31 while setting up the game icon.

**How to apply:** the only reference the user attaches when generating is their Bacon Hair image (him looking back over his shoulder). Everything else in the prompt has to be described in words. Any avatar in a prompt is the Bacon Hair (leave him out when the piece is about the rocks), and studs are allowed only on the ground.
