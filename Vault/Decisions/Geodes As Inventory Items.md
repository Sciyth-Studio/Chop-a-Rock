# Geodes as inventory items

The user's playtest notes, 2026-11-03: "geode fall should just let you pick the geode up", "geodes should become an inventory item", "geodes should be able to spawn anywhere in the zone, not just around the center so the enemy will come attack you for it", "opening a forest geode shouldn't force your camera. the animation should stay, but it will happen wherever the geode is located in the game". Reference: [[Free Camera Hatch Reference]].

## The user's choices
- **Hatch pads only.** Geodes are placed and opened on the 8 pads, keeping the hatch timers and skip product. "Wherever the geode is" means on its pad, wherever you and your camera are. Placing anywhere on the base and opening from your hand were offered and turned down.
- **Falling geodes are their own item.** Picked up with no chop and no fight, first press wins. They show as "Falling Forest Geode", keep Rare or better, and are placed and hatched like any other geode.
- **The reveal stays in the world.** Flash and burst, then the Pet stands there for a few seconds with its name, rarity and $/s above it. No blur, no click to continue, and the full-screen card is gone.
- **Geodes come first on the hotbar and in the Pets panel.** Stacks with a count, keys 1-7, Pets after them. Slots can fill with geodes first; the user accepted that.

## Drafted by Claude
These are numbers or small choices to change freely: Pet shown for 3.5 s, 30 studs x zone scale between a player's geodes, held geode at 0.5 scale, a pad with no geode in hand places your best geode (Lucky, then Falling, then the highest zone). Teleports and cannons are no longer blocked during a reveal, and you can open several pads at once, because nothing holds you still any more.
