# World 1 plan (2026-10-29)

Approved by the user 2026-10-29. Full plan with every rock, Rok, geode and number: `World1Plan.md`. Not built.

- **Zones 6-10:** Swamp → Savanna → Bamboo Forest → Volcano → Golem Ruins. World 1 is "somewhat realistic biomes". The Volcano (last before the boss) may be fantastical, and Golem Ruins may use magic. Rejected: Autumn Woods, Cave, Mountains ("in a game filled with rocks, doesn't make much sense"), Beach (it belongs before the Ocean), Canyon (too like the Desert).
- **Numbers extend the existing rules** (x125 per zone, same geode and Rok income rules); see [[Zones After Desert]].
- **Stands:** Meteor, Sun, Black Hole, Galaxy after Cosmic.
- **Savanna Roks** use animal markings and props on rock bodies, which keeps [[Roks Stay Rocks]].
- **Rok names must not clash with weather modifier names** (Toasty, Zappy, etc.); the user rejected Toasty and Sparky for this.
- **Golem boss:** chop its glowing weak spots and dodge shockwaves; co-op; gated at zone 10's toughest rock; first win opens world 2 and gives the Golem Rok. **Never make a progression milestone wait on a timer** (user rejected a :15/:45 spawn clock): it's always awake, and only repeat loot has a ~5 min per-player cooldown.
- **No Baseplate limit:** it's beta only, so space is unlimited.
- **Build only after planning**, because zones are mostly recoloured parts.
