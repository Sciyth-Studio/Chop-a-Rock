# Robux shop plan approved (2026-10-24)

The user accepted R1-R13 in `RobuxShopPlan.md` as written. Nothing is built yet, and every price is a draft in config.

What was decided:
- **Starter pack:** 29 R$ developer product, once per player. Cash worth 20 geodes, Champ (an exclusive Rok), 30 min 2x Strength and 1 Lucky Geode. A real 24 h offer from the end of the tutorial, plus one 24 h second chance after the first rebirth.
- **Personal boosts:** 2x Cash and 2x Strength, 15 min for 19 R$ and 1 h for 59 R$. They count down in play time. A free 5-min taste after the tutorial, and a "Keep it going?" card when one runs out.
- **Server boosts:** 2x Cash and 2x Strength for 99 R$, and **Server 2x Luck for 169 R$**, the one exception to "Robux luck is only the 2x Luck pass" (§00). Buying while one runs adds 15 min, and a chain offer shows in the last minute.
- **Lucky Geodes (paid geodes):** Rare+ Roks of a zone with a guaranteed modifier. The first is 9 R$, then 1 / 3 / 10 for 49 / 129 / 399. Odds are shown, PolicyService hides them where paid random items are restricted, and they're cracked at the pit.
- **Passes:** 2x Luck 299, 2x Strength 249, 2x Cash 199, Auto Open 199, Big Backpack 99, and VIP 799 (all five, a chat tag and a gold name).
- **Gifting:** any pass, boost, Lucky Geode or cash pack can be gifted to a player in the server, through developer product gift twins.
- **Other products:** Welcome back with Claim x3 (29 R$), a Mega cash pack (999 R$), and an exclusive Rok stand (1,499 R$, 3x your best Rok, limited stock).
- **Robux buttons:** one beside every unaffordable cash price. The Rebirth Skip is removed.
- **In-world spots:** purchase signs and pedestals in the world. No Robux-spent leaderboard and no Robux training mats.

**Before each item goes on sale:** run the balance model checks in plan section 11. The riskiest are the Jungle hatch-and-sell with luck x2 and x3, and Lucky Geode sell value per Robux.
