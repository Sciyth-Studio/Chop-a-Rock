# Animal Pets (2026-11-03)

The user chose to replace the rock Pets with animals. They picked natural colours knowingly over keeping rock or zone colours. Why: animals give more variety and are easier to recognise. Bloky's body keeps them as easy to model as the rock Pets.

- **Names:** Pets use their real animal names, like Fox and Bunny, never cute names like Foxy (user, 2026-11-03). Keys match the name with no spaces (`ArcticWolf`).
- **Look to match:** [[Fox Reference]]. The user called the sheet perfect. Prompts attach Bloky's plain sheet rather than the Fox's, because the Fox itself came from Bloky alone, and a fox reference could make every animal look fox-like (user, 2026-11-03).
- **Body:** Bloky's chamfered cube is both head and body, plus only the parts that identify the animal. Eyes stay copies of Bloky's ([[Reuse Pet Eyes]]).
- **No rig or clips:** the user decided the feet are too short for walking animation to make sense. The existing idle motion moves every part.
- **Plan:** `AnimalPetsPlan.md` has the 10-zone lineup (the animal choices are a draft, not approved), what else changes, and the build order. Fox first, then Forest, then the other built zones, shipping all 32 together.
- **Golem Pet stays a rock** in the draft.
- **Prompts:** written 2026-11-03 in `assets/Pets/Animals/<Zone>.md` and `Exclusive.md`: 10 zone lineups and 59 sheets, plus Capybara and King Rex (the Fox is done). Not run yet. The old zone `Prompts.md` files describe the rock Pets. Zones 6-10 lineups came back 2026-11-04, saved as `assets/Pets/Animals/<Zone>_lineup.webp`; their sheets are next.
- **Prompt gotcha:** the first Forest lineup (2026-11-03) drew the bodies too tall, so the prompts now spell out Bloky's squat proportions and say not to stretch them. The models always use Bloky's real body, but the added parts follow the sheet. A pink snout also made the Unicorn look like a pig, so it's now white.
- **Mouths:** the default is no mouth, but the user wants one where its absence looks wrong: Shark, Clownfish, Turtle and, less strongly, Narwhal (2026-11-03). The Octopus stays without one. Claude added the Koi to match the Clownfish, and the Beaver and Alligator already show teeth. The Ocean and Bamboo sheet prompts ask for the mouth even though the lineups won't show it.
- **Body exceptions:** the user let the Yeti leave Bloky's cube for a rounder body (2026-11-03), since fur didn't read on the cube. King Rex is "absolutely allowed to obliterate the body rules": it gets its own dinosaur shape with a long box snout, so it reads as a T-Rex. The Dragon may have a taller body (user, 2026-11-04). The user replaced the Baby Dragon and Young Dragon with the Lava Tortoise and Hellhound the same day, because three dragons in one zone was too many; their sheet prompts stand alone and don't attach the lineup. The Giraffe was a planned exception, but its lineup reads fine as a cube, so I'd keep the cube unless the user says otherwise. Everything else stays on Bloky's body.
- **Painted bodies:** the Tiger's stripes and the Panther's spots are painted textures, because modelled stripes looked bad and used too many triangles (user, 2026-11-03). Files are `assets/Pets/<Name>/<Name>_body.png`. They're set as the body's `TextureID`.
- **Triangle limit:** Secrets may go over 800 (user, 2026-11-03, for the Sphinx).
- **Swamp Secret is the Hydra**, the user's pick over a Firefly. The user was unimpressed by its lineup version, a Bloky body with two small heads on stalks, and chose a redesign (2026-11-04): a faceless body with three long necks and three big toothy heads. It breaks the body rule and goes over 800 triangles. Its sheet prompt stands alone and doesn't attach the lineup.
- **Jungle Secret is the Panther**, the user's pick after the Chameleon's cube and rainbow gradient looked like a blob in the lineup (2026-11-03). The user is a big black panther fan, so keep it mostly true black rather than covering it in bright spots.
- **Exclusives are the Capybara and King Rex** (user's picks, 2026-11-03), over the Bulldog and Royal Cat, because the lineup already has many cats and a Guard Dog. The Capybara replaces Champ in the starter pack and has an orange on its head. King Rex replaces Kingy as the 3x best Pet: a green T-Rex with a toothy jaw and Kingy's crown, no cape so the tail shows, and it keeps the gold aura. The old [[Exclusive Pet Sheets]] stay until the swap ships.

**Studs back on (user, 2026-11-04):** all 62 animals now use `Animal Stud` ([[Tintable Mesh Textures]]), a flat, faint outlet stud chosen after comparing a stronger version, with no baked shading. This replaces the no-stud look described above and makes the Group Model and texture ids above out of date. UVs unfold across each part's edges so studs carry over corners, with a seam somewhere on each piece. Eyes and Neon parts stay plain. The 9 painted textures have studs baked in: Tiger, Panther, Zebra, Giraffe, Cheetah, Koi, Hellhound, Red Panda and the Lava Tortoise shell (`*_stud.png`). Current ids: `Model_Pet_*` in Studio; models and textures uploaded as "Pet Animal <Name> Stud". Icons were re-rendered with studs and uploaded the same day ([[Item Icons]]).

**Swap built 2026-11-04** and merged into local `master` the same day (`aeef49f`), not pushed: see `AnimalPetsSwapPlan.md`. `src/server/Data/PetKeyMigration.luau` moves old keys on every load, with no `SchemaVersion` bump, because `Validate` runs first and rejects any version but 1. The exclusive keeps `StockKey = "Kingy_1"` and gift key `Exclusive_Kingy`.

**Golden and blue tints (user, 2026-11-04; mockup only, not built):** the user wants these versions as a tint over the whole model, not a per-part recolour. Tested in Studio, using the same lerp as `PetModifier.Tint`: yellow (255, 200, 0) at 50% and blue (40, 170, 255) at 70%. The user is fine with how they look, including yellow going cream on pale animals, except on the Tiger and Panther, whose painted bodies ignore the tint and need their own textures. Those were made by applying the same lerp to the body PNG (`assets/Pets/<Name>/<Name>_body_<Gold|Blue>.png`), set on a white part, and tested in the mockup. The user kept the dark gold Panther over a black body with gold spots (2026-11-04): Tiger gold 116221567415516, Tiger blue 128766535826170, Panther gold 84825050852508, Panther blue 82787985839042. King Rex doesn't matter because it can't be modified. How players get these versions, the saved field and the balance are undecided. The Studio mockups were removed the same day.
