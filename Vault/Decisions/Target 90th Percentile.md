## What it means for balancing ([[Balance Model]] v16)
- **Playtime 30.7 min:** the first session needs a goal and a purchase every few minutes through about 45 min. In v16 a new player reaches the Snow at about 33 min; stands land every 2.4-3.3 min. Not a check in the model yet.
- **D1 15%:** something must be unfinished when the player leaves: a long hatch, an offline payout (cap 2 h), a near Index page, the next zone just out of reach.
- **D7 2.88%:** a player on day 7 who plays about 30-60 min a day needs 3.5-7 h of progression. World 1 runs about 3.8 h (227 min) in v16, so World 2 or longer chases are needed for the engaged players.
- **PCR / ARPPU:** the King Rex scenario already breaks the Pets-share cap (57-61% from the Ocean on), which is the tension between ARPPU and fair balance.
