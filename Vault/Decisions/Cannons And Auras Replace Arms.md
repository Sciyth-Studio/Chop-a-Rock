# Cannons and auras replace arms (2026-10-18)

## Zone cannons (user)
- A lobby shop with a cannon next to it. The shop UI sells one cannon per zone, bought with cash.
- No forest cannon: the forest is walked into from the lobby (user). Cannons start at the desert.
- Owned cannons can be equipped and unequipped. The lobby cannon fires the player to the equipped cannon's zone.
- Replaces the 2026-10-03 free teleport pads, which were never built. The [[Teleport Bar]] is unchanged.
- Claude's draft, not objected to: each cannon needs the strength of its zone's first rock plus about 25 of that rock's chops in cash (Desert 500 / $80K, Jungle 62,500 / $5.1M, Ocean 7.8M / $328M, Snow 977M / $21B); a ~2 s camera-followed arc landing at the zone entrance; the lobby cannon takes the equipped zone's colour; the Arm Shop stall becomes the Cannon Shop (moot: the user deleted the Arm Shop 2026-10-18, so the Cannon Shop needs a new stall). With nothing equipped, the cannon has nowhere to send you (behaviour still to pick).

## Auras take over arms (user)
- Arms are removed (they looked lame). Auras do both jobs: the training multiplier and walk speed, with less speed than the old auras (+10 to +50).
- They work like arms do now: cash only, bought in tier order, highest owned always worn, keeping the arm numbers from [[Balance Pass Oct 18]].
- The five hand-made auras (Leaf, Wind, Thunder, Spirit, Dragon, see [[Auras]]) are scrapped. Every tier uses one shared effect, and only its colour shows the level, so new tiers are config-only. Tinting needs greyscale textures.
- Colour order (user): silver first, then the rainbow in half steps starting at true green: green, lime (yellow-green), yellow, then on through amber, orange, red-orange, red. Max saturation ([[High Saturation Colors]]).
- Built 2026-10-18 (see [[Auras]]). Tiers now: none (free, x1, +0), Silver $150 x1.25 +2, Green $2,400 x1.5 +4, Lime $19,200 x2 +6, Yellow $153,600 x2.5 +8. Later tiers continue the colour order when the balance model covers later zones.
- The red Aura Shop stall sells them. The tutorial's arms step becomes "Buy an aura!" at the same $150.
