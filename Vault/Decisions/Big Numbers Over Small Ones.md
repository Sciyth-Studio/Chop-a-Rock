# Big numbers over small ones

2026-10-10: the post-desert proposal reached billions by the snow zone. The user kept the growth: kids love feeling rich, and purchasing power stays the same as long as costs go up at the same rate. So when numbers get big, add money sinks and scale prices with income, rather than flattening the curve.

Related rule: Rok income and geode prices grow by the zone's first rock payout factor, so payback time is the same in every zone (see [[Zones After Desert]]).
