# Training stand ladder: reference prompts (2026-11-09)

20 stands in 5 builds. Wood keeps its current model. Attach your reference images to each prompt and use them for colour, lighting and finish only.

Order of work (model-workflow, sets):
1. Run the 5 **lineup** prompts. Each one shows one build in all its colours, so you approve the family at once.
2. Once a lineup is approved, run its **six-view** prompt with the lineup image attached. A recolour build needs one sheet for the shape; colours come from the lineup. Each special gets its own sheet.

All stands keep the current stand's footprint: about 5.5 studs wide, breakable at chest height (centre about 3.7 studs up), front facing the player.

---

## 1. Forged metal: Bronze, Iron, Steel, Titanium, Tungsten

```text
Using the attached reference images ONLY for color palette, lighting, and finish, generate ONE image: a lineup of 5 training stands for a Roblox game. They are one family: the same model in 5 different metals.

EACH STAND:
- A karate-style breaking stand: two short, sturdy square posts on a wide footed base, holding one breakable block laid across their tops at chest height. The block overhangs the posts and is the hero. The whole stand is about 1.7x as wide as it is tall.
- The breakable is a THIN flat metal plate, about as thick as a wooden plank: lying flat, its width is about 6 times its thickness. It has a slim raised band across its front, small rivets in each corner and a bolted bracket where it sits on each post. The posts look like short steel beams with bolted feet. The plate is the slimmest part of the stand.
- All 5 stands have the SAME shape and proportions. Only colour and finish change.
- Chunky low-poly faceted surfaces, Roblox style. No studs, no cracks, no scratches, no text. Plain flat-coloured faces.
- Flat bottom sitting on the ground.

LINEUP (left to right):
1. Bronze: warm orange-brown metal, slightly dull, darker bronze rivets.
2. Iron: medium warm brown-grey, matte, with a bright rust-orange band and rust-orange rivets. The warmest grey of the five.
3. Steel: cool light grey, clean and bright, silver rivets.
4. Titanium: blue-grey metal with a faint blue-to-purple heat tint on the edges and the band.
5. Tungsten: jet black with a cool blue sheen, and the band is a glowing white-hot orange line like a light bulb filament. The only stand that glows.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: the 5 stands from the front, straight on and orthographic, in order. Bottom row: the same 5 in 3/4 view, same order.
- Same scale for every stand. Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows.
- No text, labels, or watermarks.
```

## Six-view sheet (after a lineup is approved)

Run once per recolour build (use the middle stand of the lineup: Iron, Gold, Sapphire, Diamond), and once for each of the three space stands. Attach the approved lineup image.

```text
Using the attached lineup image, generate ONE image: a model reference sheet of the <NAME> training stand, number <N> from the left in the lineup. Keep its exact shape, proportions and colours.

SHAPE:
- As in the lineup: <one line restating the shape, e.g. "a forged metal plate with a raised band and corner rivets, across two beam posts on a footed base">.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes, chunky and readable. Keep it simple enough to be modelled in under 1,500 triangles.
- Flat bottom sitting on the ground.
- No studs, no cracks, no surface texture. Plain flat-coloured faces.

LAYOUT:
- A single image showing the SAME stand from 6 views, arranged in a grid: front, back, left side, right side, top-down, and one 3/4 perspective view.
- The 5 flat views (front, back, left, right, top) are orthographic: straight-on with no perspective distortion. Keep the camera level for the side views.
- The stand is identical in every view: same shape, proportions, and colours.
- Keep the same scale across the flat views, and align them so heights and widths match across views, like a blueprint.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows.
- No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
