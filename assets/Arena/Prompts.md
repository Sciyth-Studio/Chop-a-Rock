# Boss Arena prompts

Two lineup sheets for the Boss Arena props that are Studio parts today (see the vault `Boss Arena` note), to model with meshlab. Run each prompt in its own ChatGPT chat with no images attached; the look is described in the prompt. Same style block as the zone decor lineups (`assets/Decor/Prompts.md`). Studs come from the Studio material, so the sheets show none.

Not on these sheets: the swamp, palm, coral, kelp, starfish, pine and snow pile props reuse the zone decor models. The stands, dais rings and rim, rune strips, wave bars, ground tiles and Diamond veins stay Studio parts.

**Lineup A: Colosseum**

```text
Generate ONE image: a lineup of four props for the boss arena of a bright, cartoony Roblox game, left to right.

STYLE:
- Stylised cartoon versions of the real things, like props in a polished Roblox simulator game or an animated kids' film: simple, chunky, slightly exaggerated proportions, soft rounded edges, clean silhouettes. Not built from blocks or bricks, not voxel, not realistic.
- Stone is a plain neutral light grey with darker neutral grey trim. Accents are highly saturated: fire orange, golden yellow, ivory white. No muted, pastel, dusty or tinted greys.
- Smooth matte plastic finish, flat colour per part with gentle shading. No texture, studs, cracks, moss, gradients or fine detail.
- Soft, even studio lighting. Each prop sits flat on the ground.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Corner tower: a squat, wide round stone tower about twice as tall as it is wide, slightly flared at the base, a darker stone band around the middle, a jutting top rim with chunky battlements, and a stone fire bowl sitting in the middle of the top.
2. Gate arch: two thick square stone pillars joined by a heavy flat lintel across the top, the opening wider than it is tall. Five big curved ivory tusk spikes stick up along the top of the lintel, the middle one tallest. A plain flat rectangular panel on the front of the lintel for a sign.
3. Fire bowl: a wide, shallow stone bowl on a short thick pedestal with a flared foot, filled with glowing orange and yellow coals heaped in a low mound. No flames.
4. Chopping hand emblem: a flat, round stone medallion lying on the ground, with a raised cartoon karate-chop hand on it: an open flat hand seen from the side, fingers together and straight, thumb tucked, slicing down, with three short curved motion lines behind it. Hand and motion lines in golden yellow on the grey stone. Its front view is straight down from above.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all four from the front, side by side, standing on one ground line. Bottom row: the same four in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Props are NOT to scale: each one fills its own slot so its shape is clear.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels or watermarks.
```
