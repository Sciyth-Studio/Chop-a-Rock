# Desert Rok prompts

**Step 1: the lineup.** One image of all six, so they read as a set. Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png` plus your colour/finish references.

```text
Using the attached Bloky sheet for the body shape and eyes (not its grey colour), and the other attached images ONLY for color palette, lighting, and finish, generate ONE image: a lineup of six cute pet rocks for a Roblox game's desert zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL SIX:
- Each is a rock with Bloky's two chamfered-square cartoon eyes on the front (white square, black chamfered-square pupil, small white square glint at top right). No mouth, no limbs, no animal parts. The rock body is a bright, saturated golden amber orange (like a desert sunset glow), NOT grey, brown or pale tan. All six share this body colour.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles. Flat bottoms. No cracks, studs, or texture. Plain flat-colored faces only.
- Rarer ones look more special: more detail, brighter colours, glowing parts on the last two.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Pointy (Common): the body itself is a squat four-sided pyramid with chamfered edges and a blunted tip. No prop.
2. Spike (Uncommon): Bloky's chamfered cube with a small bright green cactus on top (one column, one short arm, a tiny pink flower).
3. Howdy (Rare): Bloky's cube wearing a slightly tilted dark chocolate-brown cowboy hat with a red band.
4. Flowy (Epic): Bloky's cube with a desert flower plant growing from the top: thick green leaves and two tall stems with big magenta flowers.
5. Ankhy (Legendary): Bloky's cube in a blue and gold striped pharaoh headdress, with a glowing gold ankh standing on top.
6. Sunny (Secret): Bloky's cube wearing a crown of glowing bright yellow sun rays fanning up and out.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all six from the front, side by side, same scale, standing on one ground line. Bottom row: the same six in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```

```text

- The body is a squat four-sided pyramid rock, about as tall as it is wide, with broad flat chamfers on every edge and a slightly blunted tip. Bright, saturated golden amber orange like a desert sunset glow, matching the lineup, NOT grey, brown or pale tan.
- The eyes are exactly Bloky's: two chamfered-square cartoon eyes (white square, black chamfered-square pupil, small white square glint at top right) on the front face, standing slightly out. No mouth, no limbs.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles. Simple enough to model in under 300 triangles.
- Flat bottom, sitting on the ground. Clean surface with no cracks, studs, or texture. Plain flat-colored faces only.

LAYOUT:

- The 5 flat views are orthographic: straight-on, no perspective distortion, camera level for the side views.
- Identical in every view: same shape, proportions, and colors. Same scale and aligned like a blueprint.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
