# Exclusive Rok prompts

Champ (starter pack, Legendary colour) and Kingy (exclusive stand, Secret colour). Both use Bloky's body and eyes with an off-white body, RGB 240, 235, 220 (draft; user's pick 2026-10-25 was "off white"). Props carry the look: Champ a red karate headband, Kingy a gold gem crown and a red cape.

Batch run: run the lineup, then both sheet prompts (each in its own chat), and send the sheets back together, saying which view is the front.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png` plus any colour/finish references.

```text
Using the attached Bloky sheet for the body shape and eyes (not its grey colour), and any other attached images ONLY for color palette, lighting, and finish, generate ONE image: a lineup of two cute exclusive pet rocks for a Roblox game, side by side.

BOTH:
- Each is Bloky's chunky chamfered cube with Bloky's two chamfered-square cartoon eyes on the front (white square, black chamfered-square pupil, small white square glint at top right). No mouth, no limbs, no animal parts. The rock body is a warm off-white, like ivory, NOT grey, cream-yellow or pure white. Both share this body colour.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles. Flat bottoms. No cracks, studs, or texture. Plain flat-colored faces only.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Champ: a bright red karate headband wraps around the body just above the eyes, knotted at the back right, with two short flat tails flying out from the knot.
2. Kingy: a chunky five-point gold crown sits on top, with glowing red and blue square gems set around its band and a small gem on each point. A short bright red cape with a thick white trim along its bottom edge hangs down the back from just under the crown, ending above the ground. The crown and cape must not cover the eyes.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: both from the front, side by side, same scale, standing on one ground line. Bottom row: the same two in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```

**Step 2: one six-view sheet per Rok.** Once you're happy with the lineup, run each prompt below in its own chat with the lineup image and the Bloky sheet attached.

## Champ

```text
Using the attached exclusive Rok lineup as the design to match exactly (Champ, the left one), and the attached Bloky sheet for the body shape and eyes (not its grey colour), generate ONE image: a model reference sheet of that single pet rock for a Roblox game.

SHAPE:
- The body shape and eyes are exactly Bloky's: a chunky chamfered cube with two chamfered-square cartoon eyes on the front. No mouth, no limbs. The body is a warm off-white like ivory, matching the lineup, NOT grey, cream-yellow or pure white.
- A bright red karate headband wraps all the way around the body just above the eyes, as a flat band standing slightly out from the surface. It is knotted at the back right corner, and two short flat tails fly out from the knot, angled down and back. It must not cover the eyes.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles. The headband, knot and tails together simple enough to model in under 300 triangles.
- Flat bottom, sitting on the ground. Clean surface with no cracks, studs, or texture. Plain flat-colored faces only.

LAYOUT:
- A single image showing the SAME pet rock from 6 views in a grid: front, back, left side, right side, top-down, and one 3/4 perspective view.
- The 5 flat views are orthographic: straight-on, no perspective distortion, camera level for the side views.
- Identical in every view: same shape, proportions, and colors. Same scale and aligned like a blueprint.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
