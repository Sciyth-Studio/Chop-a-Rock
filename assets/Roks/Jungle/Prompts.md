# Jungle Rok prompts

Body colour: dragon fruit pink, RGB 235, 35, 130 (from the user's "look to jungle fruits", 2026-10-16). It stands out from the green floor and lets the green leaf, vine and frond props pop.

Batch run: run the lineup, then all six sheet prompts (each in its own chat), and send the six sheets back together. Claude models all six side by side in one Blender scene and renders them together for feedback.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png` plus your colour/finish references.

```text
Using the attached Bloky sheet for the body shape and eyes (not its grey colour), and the other attached images ONLY for color palette, lighting, and finish, generate ONE image: a lineup of six cute pet rocks for a Roblox game's jungle zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL SIX:
- Each is a rock with Bloky's two chamfered-square cartoon eyes on the front (white square, black chamfered-square pupil, small white square glint at top right). No mouth, no limbs, no animal parts. The rock body is a bright, saturated hot pink like dragon fruit skin, NOT grey, brown, red or pale pink. All six share this body colour.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles. Flat bottoms. No cracks, studs, or texture. Plain flat-colored faces only.
- Rarer ones look more special: more detail, brighter colours, glowing parts on the last two.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Roly (Common): Bloky's cube made chunkier and rounder, with broad soft chamfers on every edge. No prop.
2. Leafy (Uncommon): Bloky's cube with one big bright green leaf on top, held up by its stem like an umbrella.
3. Viney (Rare): Bloky's cube with green vines wrapped around the body and a few small yellow flowers on top.
4. Coco (Epic): Bloky's cube with green palm fronds sprouting from the top and two brown coconuts nestled at their base.
5. Tiki (Legendary): Bloky's cube with a carved grey stone tiki mask standing on top, its carved marks glowing bright turquoise.
6. Bloomy (Secret): Bloky's cube sitting inside a giant open flower with glowing golden yellow petals around it.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all six from the front, side by side, same scale, standing on one ground line. Bottom row: the same six in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```

**Step 2: one six-view sheet per Rok.** Once you're happy with the lineup, run each prompt below in its own chat with the lineup image and the Bloky sheet attached.
