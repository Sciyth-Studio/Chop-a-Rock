# Egg lineups

Twenty eggs, two per zone, shown in two lineups of ten. Run each prompt in its own chat with no images attached; the look is described in the prompt. Plan: `EggModelsPlan.md`.

The real models all share one shell, and their colours are set in Studio, so these sheets are for props, placement and the overall look. If an egg comes out too small to read, ask for that row again on its own.

**Lineup A: Forest to Snow**

```text
Generate ONE image: a lineup of ten decorated eggs for a bright, cartoony Roblox game, in a grid of 5 columns and 2 rows. Each column is one zone, and the two eggs in a column belong together.

STYLE:
- Every egg has the SAME body: a chunky, slightly squat egg, a little taller than wide, narrower at the top, with a small flat bottom so it sits on the ground without tipping.
- Low-poly faceted surface: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles, about 12 sides around. Not smooth, not realistic, not built from blocks or bricks.
- Decorations are few, simple and chunky, with clean silhouettes, and they hug the egg. Nothing rises more than a quarter of the egg's height above its top, and nothing sticks out far past its sides.
- Highly saturated, vivid colours unless described otherwise. Flat colour per part with gentle shading. Clean surfaces with no texture, studs, gradients or fine detail.
- No faces or eyes, except where described.
- Soft, even studio lighting on a plain light-grey background. Same camera for every egg: straight-on, slightly above. No text, labels or watermarks.

TOP ROW, LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Cream egg wearing a thick bright green moss cap over its top, with chunky drips running down the sides.
2. Stripes of orange and dark red-orange in stepped horizontal bands, like a layered desert mesa, each band stepping slightly in toward the top. A small tuft of grass on top.
3. Warm tan egg with thick green vines wrapped around it and a few small yellow flowers on the vines.
4. Coral pink egg with a small branching hot pink coral on top and one plump orange starfish stuck on its side.
5. Deep blue egg wearing a thick white snow cap over its top, with chunky rounded drips running down the sides.

BOTTOM ROW, LEFT TO RIGHT:
6. Bright red egg with big round white spots like a toadstool, and two small red-capped mushrooms growing at its base.
7. Terracotta orange egg with two small green saguaro cacti growing out of its upper sides, each with one bent arm.
8. Golden orange egg with a simple carved tiki face on the front in dark brown (square brows, wide mouth, no eyeballs) and a short crown of green palm leaves on top.
9. Bright cyan egg covered in about ten chunky cream cone-shaped barnacles of different sizes.
10. Sky blue egg with a thick white snow collar around its middle and a ring of chunky white icicles hanging down from the collar.
```

**Lineup B: Swamp to Ruins**

```text
Generate ONE image: a lineup of ten decorated eggs for a bright, cartoony Roblox game, in a grid of 5 columns and 2 rows. Each column is one zone, and the two eggs in a column belong together.

STYLE:
- Every egg has the SAME body: a chunky, slightly squat egg, a little taller than wide, narrower at the top, with a small flat bottom so it sits on the ground without tipping.
- Low-poly faceted surface: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles, about 12 sides around. Not smooth, not realistic, not built from blocks or bricks.
- Decorations are few, simple and chunky, with clean silhouettes, and they hug the egg. Nothing rises more than a quarter of the egg's height above its top, and nothing sticks out far past its sides.
- Highly saturated, vivid colours unless described otherwise. Flat colour per part with gentle shading. Clean surfaces with no texture, studs, gradients or fine detail.
- No faces or eyes.
- Soft, even studio lighting on a plain light-grey background. Same camera for every egg: straight-on, slightly above. No text, labels or watermarks.

TOP ROW, LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Lime green egg wearing a thick dark brown mud cap over its top, with blobby drips running down the sides, and two short cattails growing from one side of its base.
2. Bright red-orange egg with a tiny tree on top: a short forked trunk and a flat dark green hexagonal canopy, like an African acacia.
3. Bright jade green egg with a raised ring around it at each of three heights, like bamboo segments, and three short pointed bamboo shoots on top.
4. Charcoal egg with a network of glowing orange cracks cut into its whole surface. The cracks glow brightly.
5. Dark violet-grey stone egg with glowing purple runes cut into it all around: a circle, triangles and short bars.

BOTTOM ROW, LEFT TO RIGHT:
6. Teal egg with a round green lily pad resting on top like a flat hat and one pink lotus flower in the middle of the pad.
7. Brown egg with one tall knobbly termite spire and three short ones, off-centre on top, in a darker earthy brown.
8. Pale cream stone egg with two tiers of curved red pagoda roof as its cap, and one small dark arched window on the front.
9. Rust orange egg whose top ends in a raised crater rim, with a pool of glowing orange lava inside the bowl.
10. Warm sandstone egg with deep vertical grooves all around like a fluted Greek column, a raised ring near the top, and one small patch of green moss.
```
