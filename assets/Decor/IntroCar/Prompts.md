# Intro car: reference sheet prompt

Run in ChatGPT with no images attached (Decor Without References). Built in meshlab (`tools/meshlab/models/IntroCar.luau`), so the shape is asked for as chunky boxes and cylinders.

```text
Generate ONE image: a model reference sheet of a single cartoon toy convertible car for a Roblox game.

SHAPE:
- A small two-seat open-top convertible, chunky and toy-like: about 7 wide, 12 long and 5 tall (wheels included). Short rounded-off hood in front, short trunk at the back, open cabin in the middle with two seats and a steering wheel on the left seat.
- Built from a few simple chunky blocks and cylinders: one boxy lower body, a raised hood and trunk deck, a slanted windshield in a thin frame, four big black wheels with grey hubcaps that stick out a little past the body sides. Front and back bumpers as thick grey bars. Square headlights, red tail lights, a dark grille.
- Bright saturated red paint (RGB 255, 30, 30) with one white racing stripe down the middle of the hood and trunk, light-blue windshield glass, tan seats, black tyres and steering wheel.
- Wheels resting on the ground. Clean surfaces with no studs, no texture, no dirt. Plain flat-coloured faces only.

LAYOUT:
- A single image showing the SAME car from 6 views, arranged in a grid: front, back, left side, right side, top-down, and one 3/4 perspective view.
- The 5 flat views (front, back, left, right, top) are orthographic: straight-on with no perspective distortion. Keep the camera level for the side views.
- The car is identical in every view: same shape, proportions and colours.
- Keep the same scale across the flat views, and align them so heights and widths match across views, like a blueprint.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows.
- No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
