# Cannon icon prompt (ChatGPT)

Attach `IconStyleSheet.png` (eight of the game's icons) with this prompt.

```
Make one new game icon in exactly the same style as the icons in the attached sheet: a cartoon cannon.

Style to copy from the sheet:
- Chunky, slightly isometric 3D shapes seen from a little above, drawn as flat vector art.
- Each surface is one flat colour, with a darker shade for the side faces and one or two crisp, hard-edged white highlight slivers on the top edges. No gradients, no texture, no noise.
- No outline, no drop shadow, no glow, no text.
- Bright, saturated colours, the same strength as the sheet.

The cannon:
- An old-fashioned round-barrel cannon on a small wooden carriage with two big wheels, barrel tilted up to the upper right at about 35 degrees, like it's about to fire someone into the sky.
- Barrel: dark iron grey with a thick orange band at the muzzle and one at the back. The open muzzle shows a dark hole.
- Carriage: warm orange-brown wood. Wheels: orange with dark grey hubs.
- A small puff of white smoke at the muzzle is fine; keep it simple.
- Proportions as chunky and toy-like as the basket and the cash stack in the sheet.

Output: a single icon centred on a transparent background, square image (1024 x 1024), filling about 85% of the frame. Only the cannon, nothing else.
```

After it's made: Claude makes a greyscale copy for the shop rows (they tint the icon per zone, like `AuraTint`), and the full-colour one goes in the panel header. Upload both through Studio so they land in the group.
