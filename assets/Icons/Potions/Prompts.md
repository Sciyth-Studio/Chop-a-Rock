# Potion icons: ChatGPT prompts (2026-11-12)

Five potions, one size each (`PotionPlan.md` sections 4-5): Luck (green), Strength (red), Hatch (yellow), Speed (blue), Focus (purple).

The user didn't like the Free Icon Pack's potion bottle (2026-11-12), so these don't copy it. Each potion gets **its own bottle shape** plus a symbol, so kids can tell them apart by shape, colour and symbol. The pack's five potions are only stand-ins until these exist.

**How to run:**
- ChatGPT image generation, one prompt per chat.
- Attach `assets/Icons/EventTimers/IconReferenceSheet.png` (the game's icon style). Don't attach the pack potion.
- Ask for a transparent PNG and save it here as `PotionSheet.png`. Claude cleans it up, splits it, bakes the outline, uploads and wires it in.

**Every prompt asks for:** no outline (Claude bakes the game's black outline in; a drawn one would double), a clean transparent background with no glow or haze, and the pieces well apart so they can be cut out.

## Potion sheet (`PotionSheet.png`)

```
Make five game item icons in exactly the same style as the attached icon sheet: flat-colour chunky 3D cartoon shapes, a slightly darker side face for depth, small crisp white highlight slivers, bright saturated colours, no outline, no text. They should look like toy-like blocky magic potions for a kids' game, chunky and bold rather than delicate or realistic.

Each potion is a glass bottle filled almost to the top with glowing bright liquid, a chunky cork or cap, and one big bold symbol on the front of the bottle. The glass is pale and only lightly tinted, with one or two white highlight streaks; the liquid colour is what you see most. All five are seen from the same slight three-quarter front view, are about the same height, and each has a clearly different bottle shape:

1. Luck: a round-bellied flask with a short neck and a brown cork. Bright green liquid. Symbol: a white-and-green four-leaf clover.
2. Strength: a short, wide, square-shouldered jug with a thick neck and a red cap. Bright red liquid. Symbol: an orange flexed muscle arm.
3. Hatch: an egg-shaped bottle with a small gold cap. Bright golden-yellow liquid. Symbol: a cream-white egg with a zigzag crack.
4. Speed: a tall, slim bottle that narrows to a long neck, with a blue cork and two small white wings on its sides. Bright blue liquid. Symbol: a white lightning bolt.
5. Focus: a cone-shaped flask (wide flat bottom, narrow top) with a purple cork. Bright purple liquid. Symbol: a white bullseye target with a pink centre.

The symbols must be big and bold enough to read at 48 pixels: about half the bottle's width, simple shapes, no thin lines. A couple of tiny bubbles inside the liquid are fine; nothing floats outside the bottles.

Lay the five potions out in one row, with wide empty gaps between them so they never touch. Wide landscape image. Fully transparent background, no shadow under the bottles, no glow, no sparkles around them, no haze. Hard, clean edges.
```

If one comes out weak, re-run just that one with "Make only potion N from this list" and keep the rest.
