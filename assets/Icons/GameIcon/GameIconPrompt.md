ANGLE: never flat and straight on at eye level. Use a low angle looking up for
power, or a slight tilt for energy.

MOMENT: the instant just BEFORE or DURING the peak action, never after it.

## RESTRAINT

Over-rendering reads as AI, not as Roblox. One main light, ambient sky light and
soft contact shadows. Keep out unless asked: heavy bloom, god rays, haze, depth-

chromatic aberration, vignettes. One impact effect at the point of action is
enough. When in doubt, keep the effects plainer, but never simplify the models:
restraint applies to effects, not to modelling detail.

A premium, professionally rendered Roblox game render: smooth plastic materials,
clean lighting, strong depth, clear silhouettes, high contrast, vibrant colour,
