# Leaderboard crown: reference sheet prompt

Floats and spins over the head of each board's #1 player, about 1.6 studs wide. Replaces the part-built placeholder `Model_LeaderboardCrown`. Attach 2 to 4 images for colour and finish only.

```text
Using the attached reference images ONLY for color palette, lighting, and finish, generate ONE image: a model reference sheet of a single cartoon king's crown for a Roblox game.

SHAPE:
- A chunky, short crown: a thick round band with five wide triangular points rising from it, the middle point tallest. A small ball on the tip of each point.
- Bright saturated gold. One big red gem on the front of the band and smaller red gems spaced around the rest of the band. The jewels are separate chunky faceted shapes, not painted on.
- Open top (hollow inside the band), flat bottom edge so it sits level.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles, not smooth and not realistic. Keep it simple enough to be modelled in under 600 triangles.
- Clean surface with no engravings, studs, or surface texture. Plain flat-colored faces only.

LAYOUT:
- A single image showing the SAME crown from 6 views, arranged in a grid: front, back, left side, right side, top-down, and one 3/4 perspective view.
- The 5 flat views (front, back, left, right, top) are orthographic: straight-on with no perspective distortion. Keep the camera level for the side views.
- The crown is identical in every view: same shape, proportions, and colors.
- Keep the same scale across the flat views, and align them so heights and widths match across views, like a blueprint.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows.
- No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
