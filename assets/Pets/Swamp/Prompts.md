# Swamp Pet prompts

Body colour: moss green, RGB 120, 170, 30 (World 1 Plan draft), against the murky dark green floor (50, 70, 40). Props avoid the body's green so they read: brown mud, a darker deep-green lily pad with a pink flower, a bright orange spotted frog, a red-mouthed flytrap, a dark olive-brown gator hood with a cream belly, and glowing cyan-white wisps.

Batch run: run the lineup, then all six sheet prompts (each in its own chat), and send the six sheets back together, saying which view is the front. Claude models all six side by side in one Blender scene and renders them together for feedback.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png` plus your colour/finish references.

```text
Using the attached Bloky sheet for the body shape and eyes (not its grey colour), and the other attached images ONLY for color palette, lighting, and finish, generate ONE image: a lineup of six cute pet rocks for a Roblox game's murky swamp zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL SIX:
- Each is a rock with Bloky's two chamfered-square cartoon eyes on the front (white square, black chamfered-square pupil, small white square glint at top right). No mouth, no limbs, no animal bodies. The rock body is bright, saturated moss green like fresh swamp moss, NOT grey, olive, dark green, brown or yellow. All six share this body colour.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles. Flat bottoms. No cracks, studs, or texture. Plain flat-colored faces only.
- Rarer ones look more special: more detail, brighter colours, glowing parts on the last two.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Mucky (Common): Bloky's cube with thick chocolate-brown mud drips running down from the top edge.
2. Lily (Uncommon): Bloky's cube wearing a big flat deep-green lily pad as a hat, with a pink water-lily flower on top.
3. Froggy (Rare): Bloky's cube with a tiny bright orange frog with black spots sitting on its head.
4. Snappy (Epic): Bloky's cube with a venus flytrap growing from its top, jaws open.
5. Gator (Legendary): Bloky's cube wearing a gator-head hood, like Chompy's shark hood.
6. Wispy (Secret): Bloky's cube circled by glowing swamp wisps floating around it.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all six from the front, side by side, same scale, standing on one ground line. Bottom row: the same six in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
