# Volcano Pet prompts

Body colour: fire red, RGB 230, 40, 30 (World 1 Plan draft), against the near-black charcoal floor (40, 35, 35) with glowing orange crack strips. Props avoid red so they read: black soot smudges and grey smoke, black devil horns and tail, a cream egg with orange spots, charcoal wings with orange membranes, a charcoal dragon hood with yellow horns and orange fire, and glowing gold-orange phoenix feathers. Rare to Legendary tells a dragon story (egg, wings, dragon); Phoenix nods to rebirth. Names avoid the weather modifiers (Dusty, Windy, Toasty, Zappy, Eclipsed).

Batch run: run the lineup, then all six sheet prompts (each in its own chat), and send the six sheets back together, saying which view is the front. Claude models all six side by side in one Blender scene and renders them together for feedback.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png` plus your colour/finish references.

```text
Using the attached Bloky sheet for the body shape and eyes (not its grey colour), and the other attached images ONLY for color palette, lighting, and finish, generate ONE image: a lineup of six cute pet rocks for a Roblox game's volcano zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL SIX:
- Each is a rock with Bloky's two chamfered-square cartoon eyes on the front (white square, black chamfered-square pupil, small white square glint at top right). No mouth, no limbs, no animal bodies. The rock body is bright, saturated fire red like a hot coal, NOT grey, dark red, maroon, pink or orange. All six share this body colour.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles. Flat bottoms. No cracks, studs, or texture. Plain flat-colored faces only.
- Rarer ones look more special: more detail, brighter colours, glowing parts on the last two.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Smudge (Common): Bloky's cube with black soot smudges and a tiny grey smoke wisp.
2. Impy (Uncommon): Bloky's cube with little black devil horns and a pointy black tail.
3. Hatchy (Rare): Bloky's cube sitting inside a cracked spotted dragon egg.
4. Wingy (Epic): Bloky's cube with small flapping dragon wings.
5. Dragy (Legendary): Bloky's cube wearing a horned dragon-head hood that puffs fire.
6. Phoenix (Secret): Bloky's cube with blazing gold-orange feather wings and tail, rising from ash.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all six from the front, side by side, same scale, standing on one ground line. Bottom row: the same six in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
