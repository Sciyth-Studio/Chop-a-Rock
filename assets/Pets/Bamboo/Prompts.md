# Bamboo Forest Pet prompts

Body colour: teal green, RGB 0, 170, 130 (World 1 Plan draft), against the soft sage green floor (120, 150, 90). Props avoid teal so they read: a lime green bamboo shoot, orange-and-white koi in a pale blue water ring, a pink lotus, black panda ears and patches, rust-orange red-panda ears and tail, a straw hat with a white beard and a soft golden glow.

Batch run: run the lineup, then all six sheet prompts (each in its own chat), and send the six sheets back together, saying which view is the front. Claude models all six side by side in one Blender scene and renders them together for feedback.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png` plus your colour/finish references.

```text
Using the attached Bloky sheet for the body shape and eyes (not its grey colour), and the other attached images ONLY for color palette, lighting, and finish, generate ONE image: a lineup of six cute pet rocks for a Roblox game's bamboo forest zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL SIX:
- Each is a rock with Bloky's two chamfered-square cartoon eyes on the front (white square, black chamfered-square pupil, small white square glint at top right). No mouth, no limbs, no animal bodies. The rock body is bright, saturated teal green like jade, NOT grey, lime green, blue, dark green or mint. All six share this body colour.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles. Flat bottoms. No cracks, studs, or texture. Plain flat-colored faces only.
- Rarer ones look more special: more detail, brighter colours, glowing parts on the last two.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Shooty (Common): Bloky's cube with one small lime green bamboo shoot growing from its top.
2. Koi (Uncommon): Bloky's cube with two koi fish swimming round it in a water ring.
3. Lotus (Rare): Bloky's cube wearing a pink lotus flower crown.
4. Pandy (Epic): Bloky's cube with round black panda ears and black eye patches.
5. Fluffy (Legendary): Bloky's cube with pointy red-panda ears and a big striped tail.
6. Sensei (Secret): Bloky's cube in a straw hat with a long white beard, meditating afloat in a soft glow.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all six from the front, side by side, same scale, standing on one ground line. Bottom row: the same six in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
