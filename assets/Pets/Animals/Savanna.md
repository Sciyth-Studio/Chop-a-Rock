# Savanna animal Pet prompts

Straw-gold floor (200, 175, 70), so avoid plain yellow. Prompts run in their own ChatGPT chats. Send every sheet back together, and say if the front isn't the top-left view.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png`.

```text
Using the attached grey character sheet for the body shape, eyes, lighting and finish (not its grey colour), generate ONE image: a lineup of six chunky toy-like animal pets for a Roblox game's savanna zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL SIX:
- The body is the attached grey character's exact chamfered cube, which is both head and body, with no separate neck or head. Copy its proportions: a squat cube, slightly wider than it is tall and as deep as it is wide. Do NOT make the body tall, stretched or rectangular. Keep its eyes exactly as drawn: white chamfered squares, black chamfered-square pupils, small square glints. No mouth.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles, not smooth and not realistic. Bright, saturated colours in flat areas with clean straight edges. Simple enough to model in under 800 triangles. Clean surface with no cracks, studs, fur, or surface texture.
- Each animal is clearly recognisable from its silhouette and colours. Rarer ones look more special, and the last two have glowing parts.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Zebra (Common): the body is white. It has bold wavy black stripes over the top, sides and back, a short stiff black mane on top, two small ears, a black snout, four stubby black hooves, and a thin tail with a black tuft.
2. Giraffe (Uncommon): the body is bright orange-yellow. It has big dark brown patches, two short horn nubs on top, two small ears, a tan snout, four stubby dark hooves, and a thin tail with a dark tuft.
3. Rhino (Rare): the body is mid grey. It has one big cream horn on the snout with a smaller one behind it, a wide blocky snout, two small ears, four stubby dark feet, and a thin short tail.
4. Elephant (Epic): the body is blue-grey. It has two very big flat ears out to the sides with pink inners, a long trunk hanging down from the front below the eyes, two short white tusks, four stubby feet with pale toenails, and a thin tail.
5. Cheetah (Legendary): the body is deep golden orange. It has small black spots all over, black stripes running down from the eyes without covering them, a white snout, two small round ears, four stubby feet, a long spotted tail, and a glowing yellow speed-streak trail behind it.
6. Lion (Secret): the body is golden orange. It has one huge chunky faceted mane framing the whole front and covering the top and sides, glowing gold, a white snout with a dark nose, four stubby feet, and a long thin tail with a dark tuft.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all six from the front, side by side, same scale, on one ground line. Bottom row: the same six in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
