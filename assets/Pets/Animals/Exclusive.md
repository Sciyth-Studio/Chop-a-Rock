## Capybara

Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png`.

```text
Using the attached grey character sheet ONLY for the body shape, eyes, lighting and finish (not its grey colour), generate ONE image: a model reference sheet of a single chunky toy-like CAPYBARA pet for a Roblox game.

SHAPE:
- The body is the attached grey character's exact chamfered cube, which is both head and body, with no separate neck or head. Copy its proportions: a squat cube, slightly wider than it is tall and as deep as it is wide. Do NOT make the body tall, stretched or rectangular. Keep its eyes exactly as drawn: white chamfered squares, black chamfered-square pupils, small square glints. No mouth.
- The body is warm caramel brown. Add only these simple, chunky parts: a wide blunt darker brown snout with a black square nose, two tiny round ears set high on the top corners, four stubby dark brown feet, and one bright orange fruit balanced on top of its head, faceted and chunky, with a small green leaf.
- No tail. Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles, not smooth and not realistic. Bright, saturated colours in flat areas with clean straight edges. Simple enough to model in under 800 triangles. Clean surface with no cracks, studs, fur, or surface texture. It stands on its feet.

LAYOUT:
- A single image showing the SAME pet from 6 views in a grid: front, back, left side, right side, top-down, and one 3/4 perspective view.
- The 5 flat views are orthographic: straight-on, no perspective distortion, camera level for the side views.
- Identical in every view: same shape, proportions, and colours. Same scale and aligned like a blueprint.
- Put the front view top-left.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
