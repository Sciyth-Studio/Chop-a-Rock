# Bamboo animal Pet prompts

Sage green floor (120, 150, 90). Prompts run in their own ChatGPT chats. Send every sheet back together, and say if the front isn't the top-left view.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png`.

```text
Using the attached grey character sheet for the body shape, eyes, lighting and finish (not its grey colour), generate ONE image: a lineup of six chunky toy-like animal pets for a Roblox game's bamboo forest zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL SIX:
- The body is the attached grey character's exact chamfered cube, which is both head and body, with no separate neck or head. Copy its proportions: a squat cube, slightly wider than it is tall and as deep as it is wide. Do NOT make the body tall, stretched or rectangular. Keep its eyes exactly as drawn: white chamfered squares, black chamfered-square pupils, small square glints. No mouth.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles, not smooth and not realistic. Bright, saturated colours in flat areas with clean straight edges. Simple enough to model in under 800 triangles. Clean surface with no cracks, studs, fur, or surface texture.
- Each animal is clearly recognisable from its silhouette and colours. Rarer ones look more special, and the last two have glowing parts.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Beetle (Common): the body is glossy teal. It has a seam running down the middle of its back between two domed wing covers, one short curved horn on the front, two short antennae, and six stubby black legs.
2. Koi (Uncommon): the body is white with big orange-red patches. It has a small fin on top, a small fin on each side, two thin whiskers beside a small snout, and a long flowing tail fin. It has no feet and rests on a flat bottom.
3. Crane (Rare): the body is white. It has a red cap on top, a black band low on the body, one long pointed dark beak, a folded wing on each side with black tips, and two stubby dark feet.
4. Panda (Epic): the body is white. It has two round black ears, black patches around both eyes that leave the eyes fully visible, a small white snout with a black nose, a black band across the shoulders, and four stubby black feet.
5. Red Panda (Legendary): the body is rusty red. It has two big pointed ears with white tips, a white face patch, a small dark nose, four stubby dark feet, and a big bushy tail with bands of cream and glowing amber.
6. Monkey Sensei (Secret): the body is pale grey. It has a wide flat straw hat on top, a long white beard hanging from under a small snout, a long curled tail, two stubby feet, and a glowing gold ring floating behind its back.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all six from the front, side by side, same scale, on one ground line. Bottom row: the same six in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
