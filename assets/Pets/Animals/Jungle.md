# Jungle animal Pet prompts

Dark green jungle floor. Prompts run in their own ChatGPT chats. Send every sheet back together, and say if the front isn't the top-left view.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png`.

```text
Using the attached grey character sheet for the body shape, eyes, lighting and finish (not its grey colour), generate ONE image: a lineup of six chunky toy-like animal pets for a Roblox game's jungle zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL SIX:
- The body is the attached grey character's exact chamfered cube, which is both head and body, with no separate neck or head. Copy its proportions: a squat cube, slightly wider than it is tall and as deep as it is wide. Do NOT make the body tall, stretched or rectangular. Keep its eyes exactly as drawn: white chamfered squares, black chamfered-square pupils, small square glints. No mouth.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles, not smooth and not realistic. Bright, saturated colours in flat areas with clean straight edges. Simple enough to model in under 800 triangles. Clean surface with no cracks, studs, fur, or surface texture.
- Each animal is clearly recognisable from its silhouette and colours. Rarer ones look more special, and the last two have glowing parts.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Monkey (Common): the body is warm brown. It has a cream face patch around the eyes and snout, two big round ears on the sides, a small snout, four stubby tan feet, and a long tail curling into a spiral.
2. Toucan (Uncommon): the body is glossy black. It has a white chest patch, one huge orange and yellow beak with a black tip, a folded wing on each side, a short tail, and two stubby orange feet.
3. Sloth (Rare): the body is grey-brown. It has a tan face patch with dark stripes running back from the eyes without covering them, a small snout, four stubby feet with long curved tan claws, and a small tuft of green leaves on top.
4. Tiger (Epic): the body is vivid orange. It has bold black stripes over the top, sides and back, a white snout with a pink nose, two round ears, four stubby white feet, and a long striped tail.
5. Gorilla (Legendary): the body is charcoal black. It has a silver-grey back, a wide dark grey snout, small ears on the sides, wide chunky shoulders, two big fists and two stubby feet, and glowing turquoise war-paint stripes on its cheeks.
6. Panther (Secret): the body is glossy black. It has faint charcoal-grey rosette spots over the back and sides, two small rounded ears, a small dark grey snout with a black nose, four stubby black feet with glowing gold claws, and a long tail curling up behind it with a glowing gold tip.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all six from the front, side by side, same scale, on one ground line. Bottom row: the same six in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
