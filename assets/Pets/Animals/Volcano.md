# Volcano animal Pet prompts

Near-black floor (40, 35, 35) with fantasy allowed, so nothing is plain black. Prompts run in their own ChatGPT chats. Send every sheet back together, and say if the front isn't the top-left view.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png`.

```text
Using the attached grey character sheet for the body shape, eyes, lighting and finish (not its grey colour), generate ONE image: a lineup of six chunky toy-like animal pets for a Roblox game's volcano zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL SIX:
- The body is the attached grey character's exact chamfered cube, which is both head and body, with no separate neck or head. Copy its proportions: a squat cube, slightly wider than it is tall and as deep as it is wide. Do NOT make the body tall, stretched or rectangular. Keep its eyes exactly as drawn: white chamfered squares, black chamfered-square pupils, small square glints. No mouth.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles, not smooth and not realistic. Bright, saturated colours in flat areas with clean straight edges. Simple enough to model in under 800 triangles. Clean surface with no cracks, studs, fur, or surface texture.
- Each animal is clearly recognisable from its silhouette and colours. Rarer ones look more special, and the last two have glowing parts.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Salamander (Common): the body is bright orange. It has black spots, a wide flat snout, four stubby feet, and a thick tapering tail.
2. Imp Cat (Uncommon): the body is bright red. It has two small black horns, two pointed cat ears, two tiny bat wings, four stubby black feet, and a long thin tail ending in an arrow point.
3. Lava Tortoise (Rare): the body is warm sandy brown. It has a dark charcoal rock shell shaped like a small volcano, glowing orange lava in the crater and running down the shell, a short pointed tail, and four stubby dark grey feet.
4. Hellhound (Epic): the body is dark charcoal grey. It has pointed dog ears with glowing inners, a short boxy snout with a glowing nose, a spiked collar, glowing lava-crack lines, four stubby feet with glowing toes, and a short tail ending in a chunky flame.
5. Dragon (Legendary): the body is deep red. It has two big curved horns, a long blocky snout, a row of large spines, two big wings, a glowing orange belly, four chunky feet, and a long spiked tail.
6. Phoenix (Secret): the body is blazing orange. It has a tall feather crest, a short gold beak, two big sweeping feathered wings, long flowing tail plumes, and two stubby gold feet, with the wings, crest and tail all glowing gold and red.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all six from the front, side by side, same scale, on one ground line. Bottom row: the same six in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
