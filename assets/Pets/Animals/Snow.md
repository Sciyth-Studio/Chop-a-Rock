# Snow animal Pet prompts

Near-white floor (240, 245, 255), so nothing is mostly white. Prompts run in their own ChatGPT chats. Send every sheet back together, and say if the front isn't the top-left view.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png`.

```text
Using the attached grey character sheet for the body shape, eyes, lighting and finish (not its grey colour), generate ONE image: a lineup of six chunky toy-like animal pets for a Roblox game's snow zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL SIX:
- The body is the attached grey character's exact chamfered cube, which is both head and body, with no separate neck or head. Copy its proportions: a squat cube, slightly wider than it is tall and as deep as it is wide. Do NOT make the body tall, stretched or rectangular. Keep its eyes exactly as drawn: white chamfered squares, black chamfered-square pupils, small square glints. No mouth.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles, not smooth and not realistic. Bright, saturated colours in flat areas with clean straight edges. Simple enough to model in under 800 triangles. Clean surface with no cracks, studs, fur, or surface texture.
- Each animal is clearly recognisable from its silhouette and colours. Rarer ones look more special, and the last two have glowing parts.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Penguin (Common): the body is glossy black. It has a large white chest and belly patch, a small orange beak, a flipper on each side, two stubby orange feet, and a tiny tail.
2. Seal (Uncommon): the body is mid grey with darker spots. It has a small white snout with a black nose, a flipper on each side, and a wide rear flipper behind. It has no feet and rests on a flat bottom.
3. Reindeer (Rare): the body is warm brown. It has two tall branching antlers on top, two small ears, a cream snout with a black nose, a cream chest patch, four stubby dark hooves, and a short white tail.
4. Walrus (Epic): the body is dark reddish brown. It has a big wide drooping tan snout, two long white tusks hanging down from it, a flipper on each side, and a wide rear flipper. It has no feet and rests on a flat bottom.
5. Arctic Wolf (Legendary): the body is blue-grey. It has two pointed ears, a white snout with a black nose, a white chest patch, four stubby white feet, a big bushy tail, and glowing pale blue tips on the ears and tail.
6. Yeti (Secret): the body is sky blue. It has a chunky shaggy faceted fur fringe over the brow and down both sides, two curved white horns, two big fists and two big stubby feet, and glowing cyan markings on its chest.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all six from the front, side by side, same scale, on one ground line. Bottom row: the same six in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
