# Forest animal Pet prompts

Green forest floor. Prompts run in their own ChatGPT chats. Send every sheet back together, and say if the front isn't the top-left view.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png`.

```text
Using the attached grey character sheet for the body shape, eyes, lighting and finish (not its grey colour), generate ONE image: a lineup of five chunky toy-like animal pets for a Roblox game's forest zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL FIVE:
- The body is the attached grey character's exact chamfered cube, which is both head and body, with no separate neck or head. Copy its proportions: a squat cube, slightly wider than it is tall and as deep as it is wide. Do NOT make the body tall, stretched or rectangular. Keep its eyes exactly as drawn: white chamfered squares, black chamfered-square pupils, small square glints. No mouth.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles, not smooth and not realistic. Bright, saturated colours in flat areas with clean straight edges. Simple enough to model in under 800 triangles. Clean surface with no cracks, studs, fur, or surface texture.
- Each animal is clearly recognisable from its silhouette and colours. Rarer ones look more special, and the last two have glowing parts.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Bunny (Common): the body is warm tan. It has two very tall upright rounded ears with pink inners, a short white snout with a small pink square nose, four stubby white feet, and a small round white puff tail.
2. Squirrel (Uncommon): the body is rusty red-brown. It has two short tufted ears, a small cream snout with a black square nose, a cream chest patch, four stubby tan feet, and one huge bushy tail curling up over its back, as tall as the body.
3. Owl (Epic): the body is chocolate brown. It has two tall pointed feather tufts on top, a short hooked yellow beak, a cream chest with rows of brown V-shaped marks, a folded wing on each side, a short fan tail, and two stubby yellow feet.
4. Bear (Legendary): the body is rich warm brown. It has two round ears with tan inners, a big tan snout with a black square nose, a tan belly patch, four chunky dark brown feet, a tiny tail, and glowing golden honey dripping down from the top of its head.
5. Unicorn (Secret): the body is pure white. It has one tall glowing gold spiral horn on the forehead, two small ears, a small white snout with two tiny grey nostrils, a chunky faceted mane running down its back in pink, purple and blue, a matching long tail, and four stubby gold hooves.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all five from the front, side by side, same scale, on one ground line. Bottom row: the same five in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```

**Step 2: one sheet per animal.** Once you're happy with the lineup, run each prompt in its own chat with the lineup image and the Bloky sheet attached.
