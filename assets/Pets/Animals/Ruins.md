# Ruins animal Pet prompts

Mossy grey-green stone floor (110, 115, 100), with magic allowed. Prompts run in their own ChatGPT chats. Send every sheet back together, and say if the front isn't the top-left view.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png`.

```text
Using the attached grey character sheet for the body shape, eyes, lighting and finish (not its grey colour), generate ONE image: a lineup of six chunky toy-like animal pets for a Roblox game's ancient ruins zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL SIX:
- The body is the attached grey character's exact chamfered cube, which is both head and body, with no separate neck or head. Copy its proportions: a squat cube, slightly wider than it is tall and as deep as it is wide. Do NOT make the body tall, stretched or rectangular. Keep its eyes exactly as drawn: white chamfered squares, black chamfered-square pupils, small square glints. No mouth.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles, not smooth and not realistic. Bright, saturated colours in flat areas with clean straight edges. Simple enough to model in under 800 triangles. Clean surface with no cracks, studs, fur, or surface texture.
- Each animal is clearly recognisable from its silhouette and colours. Rarer ones look more special, and the last two have glowing parts.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Bat (Common): the body is dark purple. It has two huge pointed ears with pink inners, a tiny snout, two half-open wings at the sides, and two tiny stubby feet.
2. Raven (Uncommon): the body is glossy blue-black. It has a sharp grey beak, a folded wing on each side, a fan tail, and two stubby grey feet.
3. Wizard Cat (Rare): the body is black with a white chest patch. It has a tall bent blue wizard hat with gold stars, two pointed ears poking out, a small grey snout, four stubby white feet, and a tail curled into a hook.
4. Guard Dog (Epic): the body is warm tan. It has a bronze helmet with a tall red plume, two floppy brown ears, a brown snout with a black nose, a small round bronze shield on one side, four stubby feet, and a short raised tail.
5. Minotaur (Legendary): the body is rich reddish brown. It has two very big thick curved cream horns with glowing gold tips, two small ears, a wide tan snout with a glowing gold nose ring, a dark tuft on top, a glowing gold chest emblem, four stubby dark hooves with gold cuffs, and a thin tail with a dark tuft.
6. Nine-Tailed Fox (Secret): the body is deep purple with small white star speckles. It has two tall pointed ears with glowing magenta tips, a small lilac snout, four stubby black feet, nine bushy glowing-tipped tails fanned out behind it, and a swirling glowing purple portal ring behind them.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all six from the front, side by side, same scale, on one ground line. Bottom row: the same six in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
