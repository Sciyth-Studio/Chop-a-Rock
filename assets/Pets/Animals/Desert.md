# Desert animal Pet prompts

Tan floor (160, 132, 79), so nothing is plain tan. Prompts run in their own ChatGPT chats. Send every sheet back together, and say if the front isn't the top-left view.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png`.

```text
Using the attached grey character sheet for the body shape, eyes, lighting and finish (not its grey colour), generate ONE image: a lineup of six chunky toy-like animal pets for a Roblox game's desert zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL SIX:
- The body is the attached grey character's exact chamfered cube, which is both head and body, with no separate neck or head. Copy its proportions: a squat cube, slightly wider than it is tall and as deep as it is wide. Do NOT make the body tall, stretched or rectangular. Keep its eyes exactly as drawn: white chamfered squares, black chamfered-square pupils, small square glints. No mouth.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles, not smooth and not realistic. Bright, saturated colours in flat areas with clean straight edges. Simple enough to model in under 800 triangles. Clean surface with no cracks, studs, fur, or surface texture.
- Each animal is clearly recognisable from its silhouette and colours. Rarer ones look more special, and the last two have glowing parts.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Gecko (Common): the body is bright lime green. It has orange spots on the back, a wide flat snout, four stubby feet with wide toes, and a thin tapering tail curling to one side.
2. Armadillo (Uncommon): the body is dusty rose pink. It has darker grey-brown armour bands wrapping over the top and back, two small pointed ears, a long thin snout, four stubby dark feet, and a short pointed tail.
3. Scorpion (Rare): the body is dark red-brown. It has two big pincer claws reaching forward from the sides, six stubby legs, and a jointed segmented tail arching up over its back, ending in a yellow stinger.
4. Vulture (Epic): the body is dark charcoal brown. It has a bald pink cap on top of the cube, a fluffy white feather collar around the bottom of the body, a hooked yellow beak, a big folded wing on each side, and two stubby yellow feet.
5. Camel (Legendary): the body is strong golden yellow. It has two big humps on top, small ears on the front corners, a long tan snout, a red saddle blanket between the humps with glowing gold tassels, four stubby dark feet, and a short tail.
6. Sphinx (Secret): the body is golden yellow. It has a blue and gold striped pharaoh headdress draped over the top and down both sides, two cat ears poking through, a small cat snout, glowing blue gems on the headdress, four stubby feet, and a thin cat tail.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all six from the front, side by side, same scale, on one ground line. Bottom row: the same six in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```

**Step 2: one sheet per animal.** Once you're happy with the lineup, run each prompt in its own chat with the lineup image and the Bloky sheet attached.
