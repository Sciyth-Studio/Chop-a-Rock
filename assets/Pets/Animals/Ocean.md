# Ocean animal Pet prompts

Navy floor (0, 55, 110), so avoid dark blues. Prompts run in their own ChatGPT chats. Send every sheet back together, and say if the front isn't the top-left view.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png`.

```text
Using the attached grey character sheet for the body shape, eyes, lighting and finish (not its grey colour), generate ONE image: a lineup of six chunky toy-like animal pets for a Roblox game's ocean zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL SIX:
- The body is the attached grey character's exact chamfered cube, which is both head and body, with no separate neck or head. Copy its proportions: a squat cube, slightly wider than it is tall and as deep as it is wide. Do NOT make the body tall, stretched or rectangular. Keep its eyes exactly as drawn: white chamfered squares, black chamfered-square pupils, small square glints. No mouth.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles, not smooth and not realistic. Bright, saturated colours in flat areas with clean straight edges. Simple enough to model in under 800 triangles. Clean surface with no cracks, studs, fur, or surface texture.
- Each animal is clearly recognisable from its silhouette and colours. Rarer ones look more special, and the last two have glowing parts.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Crab (Common): the body is bright red. It has two big pincer claws reaching out from the sides, and six short pointed legs underneath.
2. Clownfish (Uncommon): the body is vivid orange. It has two wide white bands edged in black wrapping round the body, a small fin on top, a small fin on each side, and a wide tail fin. It has no feet and rests on a flat bottom.
3. Turtle (Rare): the body is leaf green. It has a big domed faceted brown shell covering the top and back, four stubby flippers, a small snout, and a short pointed tail. It has no feet and rests on a flat bottom.
4. Octopus (Epic): the body is pink-purple. It has a rounded faceted dome rising from the top of the cube, lighter spots, and eight short chunky tentacles curling out around the base. It has no feet and rests on a flat bottom.
5. Shark (Legendary): the body is steel blue-grey. It has a white belly and lower face, one tall dorsal fin on top, a fin on each side, a tall crescent tail fin, and glowing cyan gill stripes on the sides. It has no feet and rests on a flat bottom.
6. Narwhal (Secret): the body is pale sky blue with darker speckles. It has one very long glowing white spiral horn from the forehead, a flipper on each side, and a wide tail fluke curving up behind. It has no feet and rests on a flat bottom.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all six from the front, side by side, same scale, on one ground line. Bottom row: the same six in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
