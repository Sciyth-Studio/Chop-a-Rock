# Swamp animal Pet prompts

Murky dark green floor (50, 70, 40), so greens must be bright. Prompts run in their own ChatGPT chats. Send every sheet back together, and say if the front isn't the top-left view.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png`.

```text
Using the attached grey character sheet for the body shape, eyes, lighting and finish (not its grey colour), generate ONE image: a lineup of six chunky toy-like animal pets for a Roblox game's swamp zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL SIX:
- The body is the attached grey character's exact chamfered cube, which is both head and body, with no separate neck or head. Copy its proportions: a squat cube, slightly wider than it is tall and as deep as it is wide. Do NOT make the body tall, stretched or rectangular. Keep its eyes exactly as drawn: white chamfered squares, black chamfered-square pupils, small square glints. No mouth.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles, not smooth and not realistic. Bright, saturated colours in flat areas with clean straight edges. Simple enough to model in under 800 triangles. Clean surface with no cracks, studs, fur, or surface texture.
- Each animal is clearly recognisable from its silhouette and colours. Rarer ones look more special, and the last two have glowing parts.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Frog (Common): the body is bright lime green. It has a pale yellow belly patch, two big folded back legs on the sides, and two stubby webbed front feet.
2. Duck (Uncommon): the body is white. It has a wide flat orange bill, a folded wing on each side, a short upturned tail, and two stubby orange webbed feet.
3. Snail (Rare): the body is soft pink-tan. It has one big spiral shell on its back in orange and brown, and two short stalk feelers on top. It has no feet and rests on a flat bottom.
4. Beaver (Epic): the body is warm brown. It has two small round ears, a tan snout with a black nose, two big white front teeth showing under the snout, four stubby dark feet, and a wide flat dark paddle tail with a criss-cross pattern.
5. Alligator (Legendary): the body is bright olive green. It has a long flat snout with a row of small white triangle teeth along each side, a row of bumpy dark ridges down its back, four stubby feet, a long thick tail, and glowing yellow-green ridge tips.
6. Hydra (Secret): the body is vivid emerald green with darker scale patches. It has two smaller chamfered-cube heads on short thick necks, one rising from each top corner, each with its own copy of the same eyes at the same size, glowing green spines down its back, four stubby feet, and a thick tail. No wings.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all six from the front, side by side, same scale, on one ground line. Bottom row: the same six in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```

It is the rarest pet in its zone, so it should look powerful and impressive while staying cute and blocky.

SHAPE:
- The body is a squat, wide chamfered block in vivid emerald green with darker green scale patches. It has NO face and NO eyes on it. It stands on four chunky stubby feet and has one thick tapering tail behind it.
- Three long, thick, blocky necks rise from the top front of the body and spread apart. The middle neck is the tallest, and the two outer necks curve out to the sides. The three heads must be clearly separated, with clear space between them.

- Large glowing yellow-green spines run down the back of each neck and along the body to the tail tip. No wings.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles, not smooth and not realistic. Bright, saturated colours in flat areas with clean straight edges. Chunky and simple enough to model in about 1500 triangles. Clean surface with no cracks, studs, fur, or surface texture. It stands on its feet.

LAYOUT:
- A single image showing the SAME pet from 6 views in a grid: front, back, left side, right side, top-down, and one 3/4 perspective view.
- The 5 flat views are orthographic: straight-on, no perspective distortion, camera level for the side views.
- Identical in every view: same shape, proportions, and colours. Same scale and aligned like a blueprint.
- Put the front view top-left.
