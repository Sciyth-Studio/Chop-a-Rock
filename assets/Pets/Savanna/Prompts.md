# Savanna Pet prompts

Body colour: terracotta, RGB 205, 95, 50 (World 1 Plan draft), against the straw-gold grass floor (200, 175, 70). The Savanna Pets are rock bodies with animal markings and props (World 1 Plan W5), never animal-shaped bodies: black zebra stripes, dark brown giraffe patches, a cream rhino horn, grey elephant ears with white tusks, black cheetah spots with a yellow streak trail, and a glowing golden lion mane.

Batch run: run the lineup, then all six sheet prompts (each in its own chat), and send the six sheets back together, saying which view is the front. Claude models all six side by side in one Blender scene and renders them together for feedback.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png` plus your colour/finish references.

```text
Using the attached Bloky sheet for the body shape and eyes (not its grey colour), and the other attached images ONLY for color palette, lighting, and finish, generate ONE image: a lineup of six cute pet rocks for a Roblox game's savanna grassland zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL SIX:
- Each is a rock with Bloky's two chamfered-square cartoon eyes on the front (white square, black chamfered-square pupil, small white square glint at top right). No mouth, no limbs, no animal bodies. The rock body is bright, saturated terracotta like red savanna earth, NOT grey, brown, pink, yellow or pale orange. All six share this body colour.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles. Flat bottoms. No cracks, studs, or texture. Plain flat-colored faces only.
- Rarer ones look more special: more detail, brighter colours, glowing parts on the last two.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Stripy (Common): Bloky's cube painted with bold black zebra stripes.
2. Spotty (Uncommon): Bloky's cube with dark brown giraffe patches and two short horn nubs on top.
3. Rhiny (Rare): Bloky's cube with a big cream rhino horn on its nose.
4. Tusky (Epic): Bloky's cube with big grey elephant ears and two white tusks.
5. Cheety (Legendary): Bloky's cube with black cheetah spots and a glowing speed-streak trail.
6. Leo (Secret): Bloky's cube wearing a big glowing golden lion mane.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all six from the front, side by side, same scale, standing on one ground line. Bottom row: the same six in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```

**Step 2: one six-view sheet per Pet.** Once you're happy with the lineup, run each prompt below in its own chat with the lineup image and the Bloky sheet attached.
