# Golem Ruins Pet prompts

Body colour: rune purple, RGB 140, 60, 220 (World 1 Plan draft), against the mossy grey-green stone tile floor (110, 115, 100). Props avoid purple so they read: a glowing cyan rune, a cream broken column, a deep blue wizard hat with gold stars, a bronze helmet with a red plume and shield, a brown spellbook with cream pages and gold sparkles, and a glowing cyan-and-white portal. Player-facing text never says "Golem".

Batch run: run the lineup, then all six sheet prompts (each in its own chat), and send the six sheets back together, saying which view is the front. Claude models all six side by side in one Blender scene and renders them together for feedback.

**Step 1: the lineup.** Attach `Vault/Attachments/Bloky Reference Sheet.png` plus your colour/finish references.

```text
Using the attached Bloky sheet for the body shape and eyes (not its grey colour), and the other attached images ONLY for color palette, lighting, and finish, generate ONE image: a lineup of six cute pet rocks for a Roblox game's ancient ruins zone, ordered left to right from most common to rarest.

ALL SIX:
- Each is a rock with Bloky's two chamfered-square cartoon eyes on the front (white square, black chamfered-square pupil, small white square glint at top right). No mouth, no limbs, no animal bodies. The rock body is bright, saturated rune purple like amethyst, NOT grey, blue, pink, magenta or lavender. All six share this body colour.
- Low-poly faceted surfaces: broad flat planes meeting at soft angles. Flat bottoms. No cracks, studs, or texture. Plain flat-colored faces only.
- Rarer ones look more special: more detail, brighter colours, glowing parts on the last two.

LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Runey (Common): Bloky's cube with one glowing cyan rune carved on its front.
2. Pillary (Uncommon): Bloky's cube wearing a tiny broken column as a hat.
3. Wizzy (Rare): Bloky's cube wearing a tall pointy wizard hat with stars.
4. Guardy (Epic): Bloky's cube in an ancient guard helmet with a plume, holding a tiny shield.
5. Spelly (Legendary): Bloky's cube with an open spellbook floating above it, pages flipping, with sparkles.
6. Warpy (Secret): Bloky's cube half stepping out of a swirling portal ring behind it, sparkles pulled in.

LAYOUT:
- Top row: all six from the front, side by side, same scale, standing on one ground line. Bottom row: the same six in the same order from a 3/4 view.
- Plain light-grey background, even lighting, no dramatic shadows. No text, labels, or watermarks.
```
