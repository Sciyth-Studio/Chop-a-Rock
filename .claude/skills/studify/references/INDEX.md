# Reference images

The images aren't sorted in yet. Once they are, each group below lists its folder and a contact sheet, so a build opens the sheet first and only then the 3-6 full images it needs.

The user downloads them by hand a few at a time into `Vault/Attachments/Stud References/Inbox/`. When asked to sort them: open each, move it into `Vault/Attachments/Stud References/<Group>/` with a short descriptive name, embed it in that group's vault note with one line on what to take from it, and rebuild the group's contact sheet. Not every image is needed; the zone matches at the bottom come first.

Groups, from the original studify dataset (142 images of other games' stud builds, July 2026):

- **Hubs, markets, shops:** island hubs with stalls and plazas, striped-awning market streets, gear shops with menu boards, a quarry lobby with portals
- **Plot layouts:** lawn plots with fences, pads and a stall; island chains; ranch and animal plots; bee farms; vendor plazas; a circular pet arena
- **Castles and villages:** castle rows with moats, thatched huts, a castle courtyard interior, a beach pavilion with piers
- **Biomes and reskins:** ice, autumn, winter, halloween, sakura, desert, underwater reef, tropical town with a volcano
- **Arenas and interiors:** a stone fort arena, a top-down river arena, a gym interior, muted sage-and-dirt arena maps
- **Assets and one-offs:** a tree, cactus and coral lineup on a grid; a stud car lineup

Matches for this game's zones: forest → plot layouts and the tree lineup; desert → the savanna, canyon and mesa shots; jungle → the tropical town and jungle bait shop; ocean → the underwater reef; snow → the winter reskins and snow city.
